Verification states

VERIFIED

Identity and relevant claim supported by current public-source evidence with provenance recorded.

PARTIALLY VERIFIED

Identity or some claims supported, but at least one material field still needs verification.

PENDING

The record has not completed the defined evidence review.

DISPUTED / CORRECTION REVIEW

A material discrepancy or correction request requires human review and a visible correction trail.

Minimum evidence record

За секој верифициран запис WPA треба да чува најмалку: record ID, институционално име, официјален/авторитативен извор, URL, датум на пристап, проверено тврдење, verification state, review note и human-review/governance log reference.

Verification rules

  • URL restoration does not equal source verification.
  • A third-party directory alone is not sufficient for VERIFIED status when an official source is reasonably available.
  • Verification records evidence for a claim; it does not create accreditation, recognition or endorsement.
  • Disputed records remain traceable rather than being silently rewritten.
  • Published verification totals must be computed from logged evidence, never estimated.
