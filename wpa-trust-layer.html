WPA Trust Layer

Јавна governance рамка за да се разликува што е изворно, што е AI-assisted, што е симулирано, што е проверено и што сè уште бара човечка верификација.

Framework active · technology-specific credentials only when actually evidenced

Core rule

SOURCE → PROVENANCE → VERIFY → HUMAN REVIEW → DISCLOSE → PUBLISH → CORRECT
Content credentials are evidence, not authority. C2PA, SynthID or another provenance technology may strengthen traceability when actually implemented. WPA does not represent a named technology as deployed merely because it appears in a roadmap or design.

Public disclosure taxonomy

Human-authored

Human authorial responsibility

Primary authored content with identified human responsibility for publication.

AI-assisted

AI assisted, human reviewed

AI may support drafting, comparison, translation or analysis; the label does not transfer mandate or responsibility to the model.

Simulation

AI-generated or simulated media

Synthetic or simulated media should be visibly distinguished from documentary evidence or a real event.

Verified source

Source-verified claim

A claim is tied to a documented source and review record. URL presence alone is not verification.

Provenance

Provenance record available

Where a content-credential or origin record exists, WPA may expose it without implying endorsement or legal certification.

Correction open

Correction pathway

Material errors or disputed evidence remain subject to human review, correction and an auditable trail.

AI and institutional authority

WPA uses human-governed AI-supported workflows. AI capability may prepare, compare, classify or simulate; consequential institutional authority remains human. External reviewers may evaluate the system, but review is not accreditation or endorsement.

No absolute “zero hallucination” promise. The safer WPA standard is verified-source discipline, bounded output, human review, explicit uncertainty and correction when evidence changes.
