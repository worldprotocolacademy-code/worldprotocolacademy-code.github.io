Submission Policy · Phase 0

Поднесување и рецензија

No submission fee · Source discipline · AI disclosure · Human editorial responsibility

Formation-safe rule: the Academic Advisory Board is currently in FORMATION_PHASE. AAB review and second-stage AAB appeals are future governance routes and are not operational until the Board is formally constituted. No final AAB decision is currently claimed.

1. Before submission

  • The manuscript must be original and not simultaneously submitted elsewhere.
  • Sources and citations must be verifiable; fabricated citations are prohibited.
  • Authors must disclose AI assistance, conflicts of interest, funding and relevant ethics/consent matters.
  • Do not submit classified, unlawfully obtained or unnecessary sensitive operational information.
  • Authors retain responsibility for every claim and citation regardless of tool use.

2. Article types and format

Research articles, practitioner articles, case studies, policy briefs, scenario analyses, methodology notes, reviews and teaching notes may be considered where they fit the Journal scope. The Editorial Office may request formatting or length adjustments before review.

3. AI-use declaration

Authors disclose material AI use in research assistance, drafting, citation checking, language editing, translation, image generation or data analysis. AI cannot be an author. Human verification of AI-assisted content is required.

4. Conflicts, ethics and consent

Relevant institutional, personal and financial conflicts must be declared. Human-subject or practitioner material requires appropriate consent and ethical basis where applicable. Security-sensitive details may be removed or anonymised.

5. Review

Submissions undergo desk review followed, where appropriate, by double-blind peer review, practitioner review or editorial review. Decisions are evidence-based and human-governed. No payment or waiver status is disclosed to reviewers as a basis for editorial judgment.

6. Decisions and revisions

Possible outcomes are Accept · Minor revisions · Major revisions · Reject · Unsuitable for this journal. Revision requests and reasons should be documented. Publication-readiness controls apply before any accepted work is released.

7. Fees

Submission, desk review and editorial decision are not sold. No payment may influence acceptance or rejection. Any future publication or production fee must be disclosed separately and may apply only after editorial acceptance under the then-current public policy.

8. Appeals

A first-stage appeal may be made to the Editorial Office according to the decision letter. A different qualified human reviewer/editor should assess the appeal where practicable. Second-stage AAB appeal is not currently operational. Until formal constitution, cases that would require that route remain pending/disputed and are not described as finally decided by an independent AAB.

9. Contact

Submission and policy enquiries: journal@worldprotocolacademy.mk.
