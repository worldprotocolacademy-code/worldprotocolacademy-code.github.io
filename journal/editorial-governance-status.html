Governance sequence

  1. Editorial scope and policy are defined first.
  2. Potential editors/reviewers are approached privately.
  3. Written acceptance and COI are recorded.
  4. Public Listing Consent and biography wording are approved.
  5. Only then is a name added to a public board page.
  6. Review activity is logged separately from appointment status.
