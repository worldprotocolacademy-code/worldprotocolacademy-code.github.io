ISSN / DOI readiness checklist

  1. Done: One stable issue landing page.
  2. Done: One separate stable URL for every planned article/item.
  3. Pending: Final accepted full text and/or PDF on each article page.
  4. Pending: Final author affiliations, abstracts, keywords, dates, pagination/article numbers and license statements.
  5. Pending: Complete first issue published on the Journal website.
  6. Not applicable: CIP is not produced for serial publications according to the NUB reply received 21 September 2026.
  7. Production step: submit the finalized article set to the NUB Bibliographic Centre for UDK assignment per article and COBISS.MK processing before final production lock.
  8. Then: Submit the ISSN application under the National and University Library procedure.
  9. Then: Activate article-level DOI registration only through a recognised DOI registration route, using each article's separate stable URL.
No fabricated identifiers. Until formally assigned, the Journal displays neither an ISSN nor a DOI. Separate URLs are technical publication infrastructure, not proof of publication or DOI assignment.
