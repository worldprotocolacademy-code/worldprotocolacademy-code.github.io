Уредничка политика

Editorial independence · Transparent peer review · No pay-to-accept · Human editorial responsibility

Current governance status: WPA Journal is in Phase 0. The WPA Academic Advisory Board (AAB) is in FORMATION_PHASE. Until it is formally constituted, no AAB review, second-stage AAB appeal or final AAB decision is represented as operational. AAB Governance

1. Editorial independence

Decisions are based on quality, integrity, relevance, source discipline and appropriate peer review. Payment, sponsorship, donation or commercial relationship cannot buy acceptance, ranking or a favourable editorial decision.

2. Current editorial governance

During Phase 0, the Editorial Office carries operational editorial responsibility under WPA Human Authority. Proposed Editorial Board or AAB participants are not treated as active merely because they have been identified or invited. Public names require written acceptance, approved biography, conflict-of-interest declaration and Public Listing Consent.

3. Desk and peer review

  • scope and format check;
  • AI-use, conflict-of-interest and ethics declarations;
  • citation and source-integrity review;
  • double-blind review where appropriate for research/methodology work;
  • practitioner or editorial review where the article type requires it.

No automatic similarity percentage alone determines acceptance or rejection; human editorial judgment remains controlling.

4. Flagship / sensitive manuscripts

Additional independent expert review may be commissioned where justified. A future AAB review route is a governance design, not a currently active step. It becomes operational only after formal AAB constitution and publication of the applicable procedure.

5. Editorial decisions

Possible decisions: Accept · Accept with minor revisions · Accept with major revisions · Reject · Unsuitable for this journal. During Phase 0, any acceptance remains subject to the Journal's applicable publication-readiness and governance controls.

6. Appeals

A first-stage editorial appeal may be submitted to the Editorial Office within the period stated in the decision letter and should be reviewed by a different qualified human reviewer/editor where practicable. The second-stage AAB route is not operational while the AAB remains in formation. A matter requiring that route remains pending/disputed rather than being presented as finally determined by an independent AAB.

7. Corrections and retractions

Correction and retraction notices are documented and linked to the affected record where applicable. Evidence, provenance and a human decision trail must be preserved.

8. AI and Human Authority

AI may assist with preparation, comparison, screening and language work but may not serve as an author, reviewer of record or final editorial authority. Human editorial responsibility remains final.

9. Financial separation

Editorial decisions are separated from financial considerations. No publication fee or payment framework is currently active. If activated in the future, no fee, waiver, sponsorship or supporter status may improve the probability of acceptance.

10. Transparency

WPA Journal distinguishes current capability from roadmap. ISSN, DOI, indexing, board participation and other external statuses are claimed only when formally obtained and evidenced.

Identifiers: WPA does not invent international identifiers. Internal WPA identifiers may be used transparently for archive and governance purposes until an external identifier is formally obtained.
