Programme Quality & Assessment Standard

Канонска рамка за професионални non-degree WPA програми: исходи од учењето, ангажман, оценување, AI-use, академски интегритет, пристапност, жалба и идно credential governance.

Current status: IN DEVELOPMENT / PILOT. This standard governs programme design and pilot delivery. It does not activate official certificate issuance, degree status, professional licensing or automatic external recognition.

2. Assessment discipline

Before assessment

  • Learning outcomes and rubric are fixed.
  • Weightings and pass rules are published.
  • Conflicts of interest are disclosed.
  • AI-use rules are visible.

After assessment

  • Result is traceable to the applicable rubric/version.
  • Material feedback is recorded.
  • Retake/appeal rights are communicated.
  • No certificate claim is made unless issuance is actually active.

3. Question-bank quality

A large question count is not by itself evidence of quality. Any WPA assessment bank should document item authorship, version, source basis, duplicate removal, difficulty balance, bias review, answer validation and retirement/revision rules. Public examples may be released without exposing the complete operational bank.

4. AI and academic integrity

  • AI may support brainstorming, language assistance or formative feedback only where the programme permits it.
  • Learners remain responsible for factual accuracy, citations, submitted work and disclosed AI assistance.
  • Undisclosed AI substitution for an assessed learner task may be treated as an integrity breach where the assessment rules prohibit it.
  • WPA AI output does not independently award, revoke or validate a credential.

5. Identity, credential and public-registry minimums

Identity & issuance

Before official issuance is activated, WPA must define holder identity verification, authorised issuing responsibility, serial/version control, completion evidence and Human Gate approval.

Revocation & correction

Future credentials require documented correction, expiry/revocation, replacement and appeal procedures. A public registry should expose only the minimum data needed for verification.

Until those controls are live and verified, certification pages remain framework/demo architecture only.

6. Canonical level nomenclature

The four-level pathway is used consistently as: Foundation · Professional · Advanced · Trainer / Consultant. “Train-the-Trainer” may describe the fourth level's pedagogical focus, but the canonical public pathway name is Trainer / Consultant.

7. External recognition

WPA does not claim automatic worldwide, state or academic recognition. Future WPA certificates are intended as independent professional records of completion and assessment. Recognition remains a decision of each external institution, employer, regulator or jurisdiction.
