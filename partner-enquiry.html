Request briefing

What WPA asks for at first contact

  • Organisation name and official website
  • Official contact person / channel
  • Relevant 17+3 category or strategic rail
  • Territory or geographic scope
  • Short description of genuine member/institutional value
  • No sensitive personal data, payment data or confidential material in the first email

What happens next

Valid enquiries enter the WPA partner pipeline as DISCOVERY / CONTACT READY. WPA may then perform public-record due diligence, ask clarifying questions and decide whether to continue a non-binding dialogue. Any proposal, logo publication, benefit activation, financial term or contract remains subject to Human Gate and the separate institutional activation framework.
