The Series

Reading Note · The Chinese Protocol Trilogy (WPA 003 → 004 → 005)

WPA 003, 004, 005, and 007 form a coherent corpus of protocol analyses of state visits to the People’s Republic of China. WPA 005 introduces the WPA Protocol Soft Power Index and WPA 007 further applies PSPI to the Beijing Convergence of Western leaders’ visits to the PRC as a measurable and transferable instrument across the series.

Recommended reading order: WPA 003 → WPA 004 → WPA 005 → WPA 007.

Reading Note · Ceremonial Diplomacy and Soft Power (WPA 006)

WPA 006 expands the WPA Working Paper series beyond the China-focused protocol case studies and introduces a transatlantic royal-state visit case study. It examines how ceremonial diplomacy, royal symbolism, presidential protocol, military honours, gift exchange, commemorative rites, and media framing operate as instruments of soft power and sovereign communication.

Recommended reading connection: WPA 001 → WPA 002 → WPA 006.

Reading Note · Enlargement Protocol and Regional Security (WPA 008)

WPA 008 extends the WPA series into EU-Western Balkans summit protocol, candidate-country hosting, enlargement diplomacy, gradual integration and regional security. It introduces the concept of protocol geometry of enlargement.

Recommended reading connection: WPA 002 → WPA 006 → WPA 008.

Reading Note · Visual Statecraft and DPRK / PRC Protocol (WPA 009)

WPA 009 focuses on reception architecture, ceremonial sequencing, visual statecraft, symbolic hospitality and public communication surrounding the Pyongyang reception connected to Xi Jinping's visit to the DPRK.

Recommended reading connection: WPA 003 → WPA 004 → WPA 005 → WPA 007 → WPA 009.

Reading Note · Thematic Bilateral, Register Economy and Visual Statecraft (WPA 010)

WPA 010 extends the series into thematic bilateral protocol analysis through the Modi–Macron engagement in Nice connected with Bharat Innovates 2026, focusing on protocol-thematic congruence, register economy and media choreography.

Recommended reading connection: WPA 005 → WPA 007 → WPA 008 → WPA 009 → WPA 010.

Reading Note · Vatican Protocol, Sacred Space and V-PSPI (WPA 011)

WPA 011 extends the series into Vatican and Holy See protocol analysis, sacred space, ceremonial hierarchy, diplomatic liturgy and the exploratory Vatican Protocol Symbolic Presence Index (V-PSPI).

Recommended reading connection: WPA 001 → WPA 002 → WPA 006 → WPA 011.
