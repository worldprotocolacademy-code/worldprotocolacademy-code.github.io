# WPA Global Editorial, Media & Social Command Centre

Human-reviewed social distribution architecture for World Protocol Academy.

## Governance
- LinkedIn is the primary professional channel; TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and other approved channels are adapted from a clean master rather than blindly cross-posted.
- The Balkans are WPA's home authorial and practical region.
- Europe is covered in full, including EU, UK, EFTA, Western Balkans, Eastern Europe, Caucasus and microstates.
- Global desks cover the Americas and Caribbean, Asia, Arab world, Africa, Russian-language audiences, and Australia–New Zealand & Pacific.
- Telegram, WhatsApp and Viber are direct-community channels.
- Google News is an aggregator/discovery channel, not a social network.
- Truth Social and Rumble remain draft/export channels until official access and governance are verified.
- No automatic publication, mass messaging, fake engagement, purchased followers or credentials in the public repository.
- Every public action requires source review, rights review, brand/governance review and **Sande Gate** approval.
- No DOI, paper identifier, WPA index, quotation or publication status may be asserted unless it is verified against a canonical record.
- Time-sensitive protocol and diplomatic facts require current competent-source verification.

## Marketing operating layer

The Social Command Centre implements the WPA Marketing Operating System:

**POSITION -> CREATE -> HOOK -> TEACH -> ENGAGE -> MEASURE -> FIND WINNER -> MULTIPLY -> CAPTURE -> CONVERT -> SCALE**

Each asset is assigned:
- one primary function: Discovery, Authority or Conversion;
- one WPA content pillar;
- one primary hook;
- one primary CTA;
- one active experiment focus where applicable;
- one landing route and optional UTM campaign identity.

## Viral Sande Content Vault — integrated legacy inventory

The previous **“Viral Sande — 30-Day TikTok Plan”** is now preserved inside the Social Command Centre as a governed content reserve rather than a separate strategy.

- Vault UI: `/social-studio/content-vault/`
- Governance / inventory: `social-studio/content-vault/VIRAL_SANDE_LEGACY_VAULT.md`

### Import rule

Every legacy idea is treated as **UNVERIFIED / BLOCKED by default** until it passes:

**Source Gate -> Rights Gate -> Brand/Governance -> Sande Gate**.

The old hooks and story selection are preserved where useful, but the following legacy assumptions are superseded by the current WPA OS:

- fixed 45–60 second length for every video;
- automatic link-in-bio CTA on every post;
- unsupported claims about leaders' intent, motive, revenge or humiliation;
- universal protocol absolutes without context;
- unlicensed YouTube / Getty reuse;
- AI avatar as default presenter;
- follower-count-based revenue forecasts;
- subjective “viral stars” as a substitute for analytics.

The vault is therefore an **idea mine**, while the Social Command Centre remains the publication authority.

## Winner Loop

Every 10 published pieces, review the relevant metrics, document what was learned and nominate controlled follow-up tests. A strong result should generate 5–10 adjacent experiments before the team abandons the winning audience need.

The purpose of the first 30–50 assets is not vanity virality. It is to build WPA-specific market intelligence about what the correct professional audience stops for, saves, shares, follows, opens and enquires about.
