ISBN: `978-608-66168-3-0`

Relevant English-book section:

- Chapter 3: **The Protocol — General and Theoretical Knowledge**
- Section 4: **Protocol mistakes**
- Section 4.1: **Protocol mistakes in diplomacy — a reason for deeper analysis or not?**
- English pagination: pp. 83–88.

The book documents the 7 September 2018 visit of Austrian Chancellor Sebastian Kurz to North Macedonia, during which an incorrect Austrian flag was displayed during the reception and anthem ceremony. The text notes that what might initially appear to be a minor error became the most important news of the visit and could be interpreted as a signal of disrespect or lack of professional protocol capacity.

The same section continues with additional examples including:
- a Swiss official visit to Ukraine where a Norwegian flag appeared instead of the Swiss flag;
- the James Mattis arrival ceremony and red-carpet obstruction;
- seating controversy involving Hashim Thaci at the First World War centenary ceremony;
- the World Cup final umbrella imbalance involving Putin, Macron and Grabar-Kitarovic;
- the Ayalon–Turkish Ambassador lower-chair incident as an example of protocol being used as an intentional political signal.
