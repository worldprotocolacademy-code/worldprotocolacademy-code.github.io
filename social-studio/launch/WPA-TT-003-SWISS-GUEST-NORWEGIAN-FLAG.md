## Canonical source

Sande Smiljanov, _Diplomacy, Protocol and Safety_ (2021), Chapter 3, Section 4.1, English p. 84. ISBN `978-608-66168-3-0`.

Source fact used:

On 14 April 2014, Swiss President and Minister of Foreign Affairs Didier Burhalter visited Ukraine. During his meeting with Acting Prime Minister Arseniy Yatsenyuk, the flag visible behind them was Norway's flag instead of Switzerland's.

This asset teaches a verified historical protocol error. It does not add an unsupported claim about intent.

## Why this has strong breakout potential

The opening is understandable without prior knowledge:

> THE GUEST WAS SWISS. THE FLAG WAS NORWEGIAN.

That sentence creates contradiction in less than two seconds. The viewer then wants visual confirmation. The video is deliberately short enough to invite completion and replay while still delivering a complete professional lesson.

No view count is promised. The purpose is to maximise attention quality, retention, rewatch potential, comments and profile curiosity.
