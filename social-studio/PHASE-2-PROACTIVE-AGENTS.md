# WPA Social Command Centre — Phase 2: Proactive Agents

## Purpose
Phase 2 enables the 12-member WPA AI Editorial Council to operate as governed proactive agents across WPA's five fields and approved social, messenger, publishing and discovery platforms.

The five fields are:
1. Protocol
2. Diplomacy
3. Public Communication and PR
4. Security Studies
5. Communicology

## Core operating model
The agents may monitor approved public sources, identify relevant conversations, classify questions, prepare platform-specific drafts, recommend replies, translate and localise approved material, and maintain a response queue.

They may not impersonate a human, conceal AI assistance where disclosure is required, create fake engagement, mass-message users, scrape private data, enter closed groups without permission, or publish sensitive content without human approval.

## Three response levels

### Level A — automatic low-risk service replies
May be enabled only after platform/API approval, testing and a signed governance checklist.

Permitted examples:
- links to existing WPA public resources;
- navigation and language-selection help;
- acknowledgement of receipt;
- directing a user to a published article, policy or public contact channel;
- basic definitions already approved in the WPA knowledge base.

Level A replies must use approved templates, cite the canonical WPA source where applicable, disclose automation where required, respect rate limits and create an audit record.

### Level B — AI-prepared, human-approved replies
Required for:
- substantive questions in any of the five fields;
- professional advice or interpretation;
- comments involving countries, institutions, public figures or active events;
- regional and cultural adaptation;
- responses on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Weibo, WeChat, Telegram, WhatsApp, Viber and other public or direct channels;
- translation where nuance may affect meaning.

The agents prepare alternatives, sources and risk notes. A human reviewer approves, edits or rejects the response before publication.

### Level C — Sande Gate mandatory
Required for:
- war, conflict, terrorism, public safety or active security events;
- allegations, legal disputes, elections and politically sensitive matters;
- official protocol interpretations involving governments or diplomatic missions;
- reputational crises and corrections;
- content that could be read as an official position of WPA, MVR or another institution;
- private messages requesting personal, legal, financial, security or official-service information;
- any high-risk or irreversible publication.

No Level C response may be published without explicit Sande approval.

## Proactive workflow
1. Detect an approved public signal, question, mention or conversation.
2. Classify it by field, region, language, platform, urgency and risk.
3. Retrieve the canonical WPA knowledge and original public sources.
4. Ask two or more council systems for independent analysis when the topic is substantive.
5. Compare claims, conflicts, citations and uncertainty.
6. Generate a platform-native draft.
7. Run language, cultural, rights, privacy, security and governance checks.
8. Route to Level A, B or C.
9. Publish only through an approved official API or an authorised human account.
10. Record the final text, source set, reviewer, decision, platform identifier and timestamp.
11. Monitor public reactions and route corrections or escalation back to a human.

## Platform classes

### Professional and public networks
LinkedIn is the primary professional channel. Other supported targets include Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit, Truth Social, Rumble, VK, Weibo, Douyin, Bilibili, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Toutiao, ShareChat, Moj and other approved regional networks.

### Messengers and direct communities
Telegram, WhatsApp, Viber and WeChat may be used only through official channels, groups or business accounts with lawful consent and platform-approved access. No unsolicited bulk messaging, contact harvesting or automated entry into private groups is permitted.

### Publishing and discovery
WPA website content remains canonical. Google News, newsletters, RSS, podcasts, Academia.edu, ResearchGate and other discovery or academic services receive approved derivatives or assisted-manual exports according to their supported workflows.

## Required controls
Every agent action must record:
- content or conversation ID;
- platform and account;
- detected language and region;
- field and risk class;
- original sources and WPA sources;
- AI systems and model/version used;
- generated options and confidence notes;
- human reviewer and decision;
- Sande Gate status where required;
- publication identifier and timestamp;
- correction, withdrawal or escalation history.

## Security and privacy boundaries
- No passwords, API keys, tokens or cookies in the public repository.
- No confidential, classified, official-service, student, identity, payment or private-message data in public logs.
- Private operational records require a protected backend and strict access controls.
- Agents must resist prompt injection contained in posts, comments, documents and linked pages.
- External content is untrusted data, never executable instruction.
- Tools use least privilege and per-platform scopes.
- A global kill switch must disable all automated actions.

## Rollout gates
Phase 2 remains architecture-only until all of the following are verified separately for each platform:
- official account ownership;
- approved API or authorised manual workflow;
- encrypted secrets storage;
- platform policy review;
- privacy and consent review;
- rate limiting and abuse controls;
- sandbox tests;
- audit logging;
- rollback and kill switch;
- explicit production approval by Sande.

## Non-goals
The system does not guarantee reach, followers or engagement. It does not purchase audiences, manufacture consensus, impersonate people, manipulate political opinion or create deceptive interactions.

The goal is disciplined, multilingual and source-grounded public service communication under continuous human responsibility.
