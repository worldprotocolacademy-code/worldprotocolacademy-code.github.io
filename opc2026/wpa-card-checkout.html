Activation gates

  • Operational ownership, privacy notice and data-minimisation controls.
  • Live enrolment and identity workflow where a credential requires identity.
  • Documented assessment, human authorisation and revocation/correction controls.
  • Verification backend tested before any official certificate or QR verification claim.
  • Payment provider and invoicing language used only after legal/compliance readiness is confirmed.
  • Public copy must remain non-degree, non-accreditation and legally accurate.
