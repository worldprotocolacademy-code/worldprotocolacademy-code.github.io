## Objective

Define one bounded institutional research/analysis question that can be answered using public or explicitly authorised non-sensitive sources.

## Deliverables

- Human-reviewed institutional evidence brief.
- Source ledger for material factual claims.
- Provenance / assumptions / open-questions record.
- Delivery log identifying only components actually used.

## Mandatory boundaries

- No credential, paywall, DRM or restricted-access bypass.
- No sensitive personal, classified or restricted data unless separately authorised and governed.
- No automated legal, security, financial, academic or institutional commitment.
- AI outputs remain drafts/recommendations until Human Gate approval.
- Pilot participation does not imply partnership, endorsement, procurement, accreditation or production deployment.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every material factual claim is source-supported or explicitly marked as an assumption/open question.
- Sources and access methods are recorded.
- Known limitations and unresolved uncertainty are visible.
- Final deliverable receives named human release approval before transmission.
