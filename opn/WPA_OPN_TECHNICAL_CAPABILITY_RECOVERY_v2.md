World Protocol Academy develops and operates governed AI-supported research, knowledge, publishing, analytical and institutional workflow systems. Professional learning is one application of the broader technical capability; it is not the sole characterization of WPA.

### What is verifiably implemented

- A public production Virtual Sande interface (`v35.1.1`).
- A production-verified Strategic Prompt Router (`1.0.1`) with automatic/explicit SP01–SP08 routing, ordinary factual-query bypass and preserved Human Gate.
- A Central Orchestrator (`1.7.0`) that can route governed work to the WPAWS executive-role layer while preserving source, evidence, safety and human-approval boundaries.
- A machine-readable architecture registry (`3.2.0`) and public evidence index (`1.2.0`).
- Clear production/staging/beta/prototype/candidate boundaries; unsupported provider, partnership, customer or deployment claims are prohibited.

### What is not claimed

WPA does not currently claim a completed external-customer AI delivery pilot, external acceptance, OpenAI approval of the updated technical capability description, or any provider partnership/endorsement beyond evidence explicitly granted by the provider.
