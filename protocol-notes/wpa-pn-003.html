Les Invalides 2026 — The Coalition of the Willing Summit and Bastille Day, Paris, 13–14 July 2026

Инвалидите 2026 — Самитот на Коалицијата на волонтерите и Денот на Бастилја, Париз, 13–14 јули 2026

Author: Sande SmiljanovPublished: 16 July 2026Bilingual MK/ENCC BY-NC-ND 4.0DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21390763

Record summary

This Protocol Note examines the integrated two-day ceremonial architecture of the Coalition of the Willing summit and the French state ceremonies held in Paris on 13 and 14 July 2026. It operationalises chrono-binding and ceremonial condensation as distinct but connected analytical mechanisms and applies Evidence Ladder+ to source-disciplined protocol analysis.

The study covers arrival architecture, bilateral welcomes, family-photo positioning, flag geometry, representation levels, working sessions, press-conference configuration, the protocol of decoration, attire and visual autonomy, and the military ceremonies associated with Bastille Day.
