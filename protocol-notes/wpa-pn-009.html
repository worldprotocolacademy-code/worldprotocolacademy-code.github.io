Publication summary

This Protocol Note develops the shift from AI transparency toward a protocol of authorship, provenance and human responsibility. Its principal original contribution is HARP-6 — the Human-AI Responsibility and Provenance Protocol, with six controls and a strict AND gate for high-consequence A3 outputs.

It also develops a Provenance Assurance Ladder (PAL), provenance of meaning, a Low-Resource Language Safeguard, a Post-Publication Recovery Protocol, a Correction Provenance Record (CPR), and a Simulation-to-Consent Boundary.

Core position

Capability is not legitimacy, and technical marking is not human responsibility.
