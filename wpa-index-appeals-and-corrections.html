Corrections, Review & Appeals

Јавна процедура за Master List / Institute Index фактички исправки, рекатегоризација, формален одговор, takedown review, provenance review и методолошки приговор.

Current AAB boundary: AAB is in FORMATION_PHASE. WPA does not claim final AAB appeal decisions while the Board is not formally constituted. Unresolved material disputes remain visibly pending/disputed.

What you can request

Factual correction

Challenge a concrete name, category, source, URL, date or other data point.

Group review

Request review under the current REV2 external scheme: A–D, G–I, R.

Formal reply

Submit a concise response for consideration alongside the relevant analysis.

Takedown / rights review

Request review where factual, rights, privacy, copyright or serious identification concerns exist.

Source review

Challenge whether a source supports the specific public claim attributed to it.

Methodology application

Challenge how a published methodological rule was applied to a record.

Procedure

01

Submit

Identify the exact record and challenged claim, provide the proposed correction and attach authoritative evidence where available. Corrections are free.

02

Evidence review

WPA logs the version, evidence, reviewer, conflict/recusal note and decision. URL presence alone is not treated as verification.

03

Decision & trail

Accepted changes receive a dated correction trail where appropriate. Rejected or unresolved material requests retain a documented reason/status.

04

Second-stage review

When the AAB is formally constituted, eligible second-stage appeals may be referred under its published mandate and COI rules. Until then, no AAB final determination is claimed.

Current safeguards

  • No payment can improve an analytical outcome.
  • No disputed candidate score should be represented as a final independent determination.
  • Human Authority remains responsible for consequential public decisions.
  • Missing data is not automatically treated as negative evidence.
  • The programme is not accreditation, legal recognition or official ranking.
