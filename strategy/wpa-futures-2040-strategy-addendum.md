# WPA FUTURES 2040 — Strategy Addendum

**Title:** Human Authority, Protocol and Institutional Governance in the Age of AI  
**Status:** Research Development / Pilot  
**Date:** 5 October 2026

## Strategic purpose

WPA Institute will develop a governed foresight capability that does not attempt to predict one future. It will construct and compare alternative futures, test institutional legitimacy under changing technology, and convert evidence-backed findings into bounded actions.

The strategic sequence is:

**Horizon Scan -> Research Question -> Alternative Scenarios -> Structured Expert Assessment -> Workshop -> Action Cards -> AI PROTOCOL Stress Test -> Human-Reviewed Recommendations -> Measurement -> Correction -> Next Cycle**

This approach is informed by established participatory foresight and scenario practice, including the scenario/expert/workshop/action logic used by The Millennium Project. No partnership, endorsement or affiliation is implied.

## Three initial WPA institutional AI scenarios

### A. Managed Human-AI Institutions
AI becomes materially more capable while institutions preserve explicit human authority, bounded mandate, provenance, temporal authorisation, Human Gates and final human responsibility.

### B. Protocol Without Authority
Institutions remain formally human-led while AI becomes the de facto source of decisions, communications and operational sequencing without equivalent visibility of mandate and responsibility.

### C. Institutional Legitimacy Crisis
Persistent and increasingly autonomous systems operate across institutional boundaries while authority, revocation, provenance and final responsibility become difficult to reconstruct.

## Seven-question institutional stress test

Every major scenario and institutional solution should ask:

1. **Authority** — who has the legitimate right to decide?
2. **Mandate** — who authorised the system to act?
3. **Scope** — what exactly may it do?
4. **Time** — when does that authority expire?
5. **Provenance** — can the instruction and decision chain be demonstrated?
6. **Human Gate** — where is attributable human authorisation mandatory?
7. **Responsibility** — who remains finally institutionally accountable?

## Strategic functions to develop

### WPA Institutional Futures Observatory
A public-source, evidence-governed horizon-scanning function across AI/AGI, protocol, diplomacy, strategic communication, security, communicology, disinformation, autonomous agents, robotics and other emerging technologies.

### WPA International Expert Network
A separately consented pool of academics, diplomats, protocol professionals, security practitioners, communicators, AI researchers, legal/governance experts and foresight practitioners. Journal authors, lecturers and AAB participants do not automatically become members.

### WPA Institutional AI Action Cards
A structured action format scoring expected impact, feasibility, urgency, human-authority risk, implementation horizon, dependencies, Human Gate and required evidence.

### Scenario-based education
Foresight becomes a teaching method through Scenario Lab, case simulations, institutional stress tests and strategic-foresight modules for protocol, diplomacy, public communication and institutional leadership.

## 12-18 month pilot concept

1. Literature and horizon scan.
2. International expert panel formation.
3. Three alternative institutional-AI scenarios.
4. Structured expert assessment / Delphi-compatible design where appropriate.
5. North Macedonia thematic workshop.
6. Regional workshop only if partners/funding are confirmed.
7. 20-30 Institutional AI Action Cards.
8. Institutional AI Governance Report.
9. AI PROTOCOL policy brief.
10. Scenario-based educational/media output.

## Funding position

The preferred external-funding proposition is not “support the printing of a book.” It is a public-interest **WPA Institute Programme on Human Authority & AI Governance**, with AI PROTOCOL publication/dissemination as one deliverable alongside expert review, policy briefs, international dialogue, workshops, research and educational outputs.

All funder, partner, sponsor, expert-network and event claims remain unconfirmed unless separately evidenced and human-approved.
