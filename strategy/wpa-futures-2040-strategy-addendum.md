# WPA FUTURES 2040 — Strategy Addendum

**Title:** Human Authority, Protocol and Institutional Governance in the Age of AI
**Status:** Research Development / Pilot
**Date:** 5 October 2026

## Strategic purpose

WPA Institute will develop a governed foresight capability that does not attempt to predict one future. It will construct and compare alternative futures, test institutional legitimacy under changing technology, and convert evidence-backed findings into bounded actions.

The strategic sequence is:

**Horizon Scan -> Research Question -> Alternative Scenarios -> Structured Expert Assessment -> Workshop -> Action Cards -> AI PROTOCOL Stress Test -> Human-Reviewed Recommendations -> Measurement -> Correction -> Next Cycle**

This approach is informed by established participatory foresight and scenario practice, including the scenario/expert/workshop/action logic used by The Millennium Project. No partnership, endorsement or affiliation is implied.

## Three initial WPA institutional AI scenarios

### A. Managed Human-AI Institutions
AI becomes materially more capable while institutions preserve explicit human authority, bounded mandate, provenance, temporal authorisation, Human Gates and final human responsibility.

### B. Protocol Without Authority
Institutions remain formally human-led while AI becomes the de facto source of decisions, communications and operational sequencing without equivalent visibility of mandate and responsibility.

### C. Institutional Legitimacy Crisis
Persistent and increasingly autonomous systems operate across institutional boundaries while authority, revocation, provenance and final responsibility become difficult to reconstruct.

## Seven-question institutional stress test

Every major scenario and institutional solution should ask:

1. **Authority** — who has the legitimate right to decide?
2. **Mandate** — who authorised the system to act?
3. **Scope** — what exactly may it do?
4. **Time** — when does that authority expire?
5. **Provenance** — can the instruction and decision chain be demonstrated?
6. **Human Gate** — where is attributable human authorisation mandatory?
7. **Responsibility** — who remains finally institutionally accountable?

## Strategic functions to develop

### WPA Institutional Futures Observatory
A public-source, evidence-governed horizon-scanning function across AI/AGI, protocol, diplomacy, strategic communication, security, communicology, disinformation, autonomous agents, robotics and other emerging technologies.

### WPA International Expert Network
A separately consented pool of academics, diplomats, protocol professionals, security practitioners, communicators, AI researchers, legal/governance experts and foresight practitioners. Journal authors, lecturers and AAB participants do not automatically become members.

### WPA Institutional AI Action Cards
A structured action format scoring expected impact, feasibility, urgency, human-authority risk, implementation horizon, dependencies, Human Gate and required evidence.

### Scenario-based education
Foresight becomes a teaching method through Scenario Lab, case simulations, institutional stress tests and strategic-foresight modules for protocol, diplomacy, public communication and institutional leadership.

## Funding position

The preferred external-funding proposition is not “support the printing of a book.” It is a public-interest **WPA Institute Programme on Human Authority & AI Governance**, with AI PROTOCOL publication/dissemination as one deliverable alongside expert review, policy briefs, international dialogue, workshops, research and educational outputs.

All funder, partner, sponsor, expert-network and event claims remain unconfirmed unless separately evidenced and human-approved.


## WIIE integration

WPA FUTURES 2040 is not a standalone forecasting island. It is the **foresight and institutional stress-test component inside the WPA Institutional Intelligence Engine (WIIE)**.

For major WPA problems, WIIE routes the proposed solution through the 2030/2035/2040 horizons, alternative scenarios, second- and third-order consequences, institutional legitimacy questions and the seven-part AI PROTOCOL stress test before consequential implementation.

The relationship is:

**WPA Institutional Operating Protocol -> WIIE -> specialist profiles / evidence -> WPA FUTURES 2040 -> AI PROTOCOL Gate -> Human Gate -> implementation -> adversarial review -> verification -> governed learning.**

This preserves the distinction between **scenario analysis** and **prediction**, and between **machine capability** and **institutional authority**.

## 16 October 2026 doctrine extension — AI PROTOCOL 2030–2040

This addendum is extended with a rights-and-governance layer for the transition from AI tools to increasingly agentic institutional systems.

### Strategic doctrine

WPA will treat 2030–2040 as a governance horizon rather than a prediction exercise. The core research question is not simply what AI will be able to do, but whether institutions retain legitimate human authority, visible responsibility, traceable provenance and meaningful human recourse as AI capability expands.

### Five additional research lines

1. **Agentic transition** — governance of the shift from answer-generating systems to planning, tool-using and action-taking AI agents.
2. **Right to Human Presence** — conditions under which a person must be able to obtain accountable human review of a consequential AI-mediated process.
3. **Verified digital provenance** — authentication of consequential voices, images, documents, instructions and synthetic media.
4. **Intergenerational AI responsibility** — long-horizon effects of present-day infrastructure, data and automation choices on future autonomy and institutional legitimacy.
5. **AI literacy as civic literacy** — practical ability to question outputs, verify sources, understand uncertainty and recognise when human escalation is required.

### Right to Human Presence

WPA adopts the following research proposition for development and testing:

> No technological efficiency should completely extinguish the right of a human being to be heard by a human being where a consequential institutional process materially affects rights, safety, legal position, health, access to essential services or institutional status.

The strength of this safeguard rises with consequence. Human review should be mandatory or presumptively available where there is irreversible impact, material rights effect, contested facts, uncertainty, vulnerability, opaque reasoning or a legal/institutional duty of accountable discretion.

### WPA 2040 Test

In addition to the existing seven-question institutional stress test, WPA will apply a compact rights-facing test:

1. **Human dignity** — does the system preserve the dignity of the person affected?
2. **Challenge** — can the person meaningfully contest the result?
3. **Responsibility** — is it clear who remains accountable?
4. **Traceability** — can the decision and source chain be reconstructed?
5. **Future acceptability** — would we accept the same rule if it governed our own children or successors in 2040?

A negative answer creates a presumption that the design requires additional safeguards, narrower scope or human review before consequential deployment.

### Programme identity

The public-facing initiative may be presented as:

**WPA FUTURES 2030–2040 — Global Protocol, AI Governance & Human Dignity Programme**

This is a research and educational programme identity inside the existing WPA FUTURES 2040 architecture; it does not create a separate legal entity, accreditation claim or autonomous institutional structure.

### Updated strategic proposition

> The protocol of the future will regulate not only relations between human beings and between states, but also relations between human and machine, institution and algorithm, and state and artificial intelligence.

### Updated WIIE routing

For high-consequence institutional proposals, WIIE should test:

**evidence -> scenario horizon -> agentic-risk assessment -> provenance -> WPA 2040 Test -> AI PROTOCOL Gate -> Human Gate -> bounded implementation -> monitoring -> correction -> governed learning**
