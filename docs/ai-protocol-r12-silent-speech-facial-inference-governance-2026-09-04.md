# WPA / Institute - AI Authority, Rights & Remedies Framework v1.3

**Implemented update:** R12 - Silent Speech, Facial Inference and Pre-Expressive Human Authority  
**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Institution:** World Protocol Academy / Institute for Protocol, Diplomacy, Public Communication & Security Studies

## R12 scope

R12 adds a governance layer for silent-speech, facial-micro-movement and biosignal inference systems. It complements R11 by addressing not how long an AI agent may act, but how deeply an AI system may infer before a human has spoken, consented or issued a command.

## Additional directive principles

### 12. Pre-Expressive Signal Protection Directive
Facial micro-movements, whispers, breath patterns and sub-vocal signals are protected human-context signals. Detectability is not declaration. Institutional use must define purpose, scope, signal minimisation, consent, evidence, refusal and remedy.

### 13. Silent Speech Consent & Command Gate
A silent, whispered or mouthed signal may become a command only when intentionality, confidence, context and consequence have been validated. Incidental movement, emotional expression, rehearsal, disability-related motion or low-confidence inference must default to **no command** and **no irreversible action**.

### 14. Facial and Biosignal Inference Minimality Directive
Emotion, pulse, respiration, fatigue, identity, stress or health-related inferences must not be bundled into ordinary interface use. Each biosignal inference requires separate purpose justification, retention rule, access boundary and remedy path.

### 15. Assistive-Surveillance Boundary Directive
Silent-speech and facial-inference tools may support accessibility, privacy and communication in difficult environments. The same capability becomes surveillance when it extracts meaning, emotion or intent without meaningful awareness, choice, proportionality and human remedy.

## Operational checklist for silent-speech and facial-inference systems

1. Is the use assistive, private, professional, institutional or surveillance-related?
2. Which signals are captured: facial movement, whisper, breath, audio, identity, emotion, pulse, respiration or health indicators?
3. Which signals are strictly necessary for the authorised function?
4. Can the system distinguish intentional command from incidental movement, rehearsal, disability, emotion or low-confidence inference?
5. Does uncertainty produce a no-command / no-action default?
6. Are emotion, identity or health-related inferences separately authorised and logged?
7. Can the person refuse, pause, revoke, delete, challenge or obtain remedy?
8. Does the evidence record preserve signal type, purpose, confidence, human review and consequence?

## R12 signature rule

**The face may become an interface; it must not become an involuntary mandate. Silent expression is not silent consent.**

Македонски:

**Лицето може да стане интерфејс; не смее да стане неволен мандат. Тивкиот израз не е тивка согласност.**
