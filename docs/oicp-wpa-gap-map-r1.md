## Highest-value lessons to absorb now

### 1. Institutional continuity must become a designed capability

WPA has strong systems architecture but is young. OICP's principal lesson is that professional legitimacy compounds through continuity. WPA should explicitly design for institutional memory, version history, stewardship, succession and continuity beyond any single platform, tool or person.

### 2. Professional ethics should have a visible institutional home

WPA already has stronger machine/governance controls in the Human Gate. The lesson from OICP is organisational: give professional ethics/deontology a recognizable institutional function so external users can understand where questions of conduct, conflicts, professional integrity and good practice are handled.

### 3. A global protocol atlas is strategically central

OICP validates the demand for country-by-country protocol knowledge. WPA should not merely reproduce country pages. It should create a provenance-aware, updateable, machine-readable and correction-capable global protocol atlas.

### 4. Congresses should build the institution

OICP's long congress history shows the value of recurring gathering as a continuity mechanism. WPA should require every major event to feed research, publications, curriculum, network mapping and future agenda-setting.

### 5. Internationalisation should begin with contribution

The right posture toward OICP and similar bodies is: bring evidence, country knowledge, research, comparative insight and digital-protocol expertise before asking for status, membership or formal partnership.

## Areas where WPA should deliberately not imitate OICP

1. Do not activate a professional registry, accreditation or recognition system merely because a peer institution has one.
2. Do not treat historical prestige as a substitute for source-level evidence.
3. Do not adopt a structure simply to look more institutional; every WPA body must have a real function, owner, inputs, outputs and Human Gate.
4. Do not copy country-page expression or proprietary materials; abstract the public institutional principle and build a WPA-native evidence system.
5. Do not let international partnership ambition weaken the existing Human Gate or evidence standards.
