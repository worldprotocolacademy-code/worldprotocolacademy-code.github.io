## 1. Executive premise

The work invested in AI Advokat and Sazdo–Popac Digital Archive must not be treated as two isolated favours or one-off website builds. Together they form the first reusable evidence base for a WPA service line: **human-gated digital knowledge architecture**.

The economic objective is to convert accumulated research, architecture, governance, editorial discipline and technical implementation into reusable WPA intellectual assets, recurring revenue opportunities, professional references and future client acquisition — while leaving each client's authorship, content ownership and substantive professional responsibility with the client/rightsholder.

The operating formula is:

`client knowledge/content + WPA digital architecture + provenance + governance + Human Gate + controlled release = reusable professional platform`

## 2. What WPA has actually created

Across the two pilot projects WPA has developed reusable capabilities rather than only pages and visual design:

1. information architecture and content modelling;
2. source/provenance discipline;
3. version and status control;
4. Human Gate decision logic;
5. publication and release-state separation;
6. rights-status and attribution controls;
7. audit trails and review workflows;
8. controlled AI/RAG readiness;
9. security-aware static/digital platform patterns;
10. professional presentation, metadata and discoverability;
11. repeatable client onboarding and closeout logic;
12. reusable governance registries, checklists and templates.

These reusable elements are the basis of WPA's own know-how and may be adapted for future projects without transferring or republishing client-owned content.

## 11. Recommended commercial terms architecture

Every future client engagement should distinguish:

1. **Scope** — exactly what WPA builds.
2. **Client content ownership** — client/rightsholder ownership remains explicit.
3. **WPA reusable methodology** — generic framework/know-how is not automatically transferred exclusively.
4. **Third-party rights** — remain subject to their own licences.
5. **Hosting/domain** — who owns and pays.
6. **Maintenance** — what is included and for how long.
7. **Update requests** — included quota or separate pricing.
8. **Security/backup responsibility** — explicit.
9. **AI/RAG use** — separate opt-in gate.
10. **Public credit** — agreed wording.
11. **Commercial rights** — fees, licence or revenue share only if written.
12. **Exit/transfer** — what happens if collaboration ends.

A lawyer/accountant should review the final contractual and tax implementation before paid activation.

## 12. WPA credit standard

## 15. Suggested sales rule

**Friendship may reduce the price; it should not erase the value.**

Possible categories:
- founding/pilot: free or symbolic only by explicit decision;
- professional friend/referral: preferential price;
- standard client: standard package;
- institutional client: scoped quotation.

Every zero-fee engagement should state the strategic consideration WPA receives, for example portfolio rights, testimonial permission, case-study permission, or pilot/R&D value. This prevents invisible uncompensated labour from becoming the default.

## 18. Governance gates

Current WPA commercial architecture remains fail-closed.

Separate Human Gates are required for:
- public claim of formal partnership;
- logo use where permission is needed;
- contract execution;
- pricing commitment;
- payment activation;
- revenue share;
- handling client personal/confidential information;
- AI/RAG ingestion;
- institutional licence;
- public case study beyond already public facts.

Project collaboration status does not automatically create a formal commercial partnership.

## 20. Strategic conclusion

The greatest return to WPA is not a one-off payment from either current pilot. The greater asset is the conversion of two intensive projects into a **repeatable professional product family**.

The practical doctrine is:

**Do the work once → document the method → retain the reusable architecture → credit WPA accurately → convert the next implementation into paid scope → attach recurring maintenance → build case studies → generate referrals → scale selectively.**

WPA should remain academically credible while making its technical and intellectual labour economically sustainable.
