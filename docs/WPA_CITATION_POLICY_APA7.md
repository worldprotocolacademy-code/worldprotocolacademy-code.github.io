# WPA Citation Policy — APA 7th Edition

Status: CANONICAL CITATION STYLE  
Effective date: 18 November 2026

World Protocol Academy uses **APA 7th edition (author–date)** as the canonical reference style for academic publication displays and export-ready citations.

## Rules

- Preserve the exact published title and language.
- Use descriptive English translations only as clearly labelled discovery metadata when no English title is confirmed as published.
- Journal article reference pattern: Author. (Year). Article title. *Journal Title, volume*(issue), pages. DOI/URL only when applicable under APA 7.
- ISSN, UDK, ORCID and other identifiers remain bibliographic metadata; they are not appended to the APA reference unless a specific external platform requires them.
- BibTeX and RIS remain supported interchange/export formats; they do not replace the canonical APA 7 display citation.
- Historical file names are preserved for link stability and are not renumbered solely to match later Scholar record identifiers.

## 2026 Security paper

Canonical APA 7 citation:

Smiljanov, S. (2026). Институционалниот протокол и дипломатската симболика: од СФРЈ до постмодерната македонска дипломатска практика. Безбедност / Security, LXVII(11), 170–178.

The WPA Scholar record is `/scholar/p21.html`; the plain-text APA export is `/scholar/p21.apa.txt`.

## Rights boundary

A bibliographic record may be public even when the source PDF is not redistributed. WPA does not republish a PDF when the supplied source states that reproduction or transformation requires permission from the publisher and authors, unless that permission is evidenced.
