# WPA Founder Continuity & Human Succession Constitution

**R16 · Founder Continuity, Human Succession and Post-Founder AI Governance**  
**Date:** 12 September 2026  
**Founder:** Sande Smiljanov  
**Primary designated human successor:** Vera-Patricia Smiljanov / Вера-Патрициа Смиљанов  
**Status:** Founder-designated internal governance constitution pending separate legal/notarial formalisation under applicable law.

---

## 1. Constitutional purpose

This Constitution defines how the World Protocol Academy (WPA), its Institute, doctrine, publications, digital systems, AI systems, archives and institutional functions should continue after the Founder’s death, permanent incapacity or voluntary succession.

Its governing proposition is:

> **Institutional continuity may survive the Founder; the Founder’s personal human authority does not transfer to a machine.**

WPA shall remain human-governed. No AI system, digital twin, avatar, language model, autonomous agent or synthetic representation may become the legal, institutional or doctrinal successor to the Founder.

---

## 2. Founder status and permanent authorship

Sande Smiljanov remains permanently identified as Founder and original author of the Founder Canon.

Founder authorship is non-transferable. Succession transfers institutional responsibility and future decision authority; it does not transfer authorship of the Founder’s works, personal identity, biography, voice, intentions or historical decisions.

No successor, AI system or institutional body may retroactively alter the historical record of what the Founder wrote, approved, published or decided.

---

## 3. Founder Canon Lock

All works, doctrines, books, papers, canonical policy texts and machine-readable governance records personally approved by the Founder before the succession trigger may be designated **Founder-Authenticated Canon**.

Founder-Authenticated Canon must remain immutable as an historical and authorial record.

Future development may occur only through clearly labelled instruments such as:

- Commentary Edition;
- Successor Edition;
- Post-Founder Supplement;
- Institutional Update;
- Research Revision;
- New WPA Doctrine adopted under valid post-Founder Human Authority.

No such later work may be presented as a new statement personally made or approved by Sande Smiljanov after his death or permanent incapacity.

---

## 4. Primary Human Successor

The Founder designates **Vera-Patricia Smiljanov / Вера-Патрициа Смиљанов** as the **Primary Human Successor Authority** for WPA, subject to formal legal implementation and her acceptance of the role.

Her post-activation institutional role should be described as one or more of the following, according to the applicable legal and organisational form:

- Institutional Successor of the World Protocol Academy;
- Custodian of the Founder Canon;
- President / Director / Governing Human Authority of WPA, where legally and organisationally applicable;
- Human Authority responsible for the post-Founder Human Gate.

She does not become the Founder and does not inherit the Founder’s personal authorship. She becomes the living human authority responsible for governing WPA after valid succession activation.

---

## 5. Succession trigger events

A succession trigger may occur only upon one of the following:

1. verified death of the Founder;
2. verified permanent incapacity preventing the Founder from exercising institutional authority;
3. explicit voluntary succession executed by the Founder while capable of giving valid authorisation.

No AI inference, inactivity timer, missing login, health prediction, biometric signal, social-media report or third-party rumour may activate succession.

Succession activation requires documentary verification appropriate to the trigger, human review and an auditable activation record.

---

## 6. Post-Founder Virtual Sande

Virtual Sande may continue to operate after a valid succession trigger, but only in **Founder Continuity Mode**.

In Founder Continuity Mode, Virtual Sande may:

- retrieve and explain Founder-Authenticated Canon;
- preserve institutional memory and provenance;
- provide source-grounded research and historical interpretation;
- assist the current Human Authority with analysis, comparison, drafting and institutional continuity;
- distinguish Founder-authenticated material from post-Founder interpretation;
- support archives, education, research and public explanation.

Virtual Sande may not:

- claim to be Sande Smiljanov as a living or continuing human person;
- claim new personal memories, wishes, opinions or decisions of the Founder;
- issue new Founder commands;
- create new Founder-authored doctrine;
- approve contracts, financial obligations, appointments, credentials, partnerships or legal commitments;
- alter the Founder Canon;
- appoint its own Human Authority;
- override Vera-Patricia Smiljanov or any later valid Human Authority;
- represent synthetic inference as the Founder’s actual posthumous intent.

Canonical post-Founder disclosure:

> **Virtual Sande is an AI institutional-memory and advisory system based on authorised WPA materials. It is not Sande Smiljanov, does not continue his personal authority, and cannot speak new decisions in his name.**

---

## 7. Human Gate after succession

After valid activation, consequential WPA actions must remain attributable to a living authorised human.

The post-Founder authority chain is:

**Founder Canon -> Current Human Authority -> Human Gate -> AI Assistance -> Institutional Action -> Evidence -> Accountability**

Virtual Sande, WPAWS, the WPA AI Council and any future agents remain subordinate to the Human Gate.

A remembered Founder instruction is historical context unless it was expressly designed to survive as a continuing institutional rule and remains lawful and applicable.

---

## 8. Reserved constitutional matters

The following matters must never be decided by AI alone and should require enhanced human governance:

- amendment or weakening of Human Constitutional Primacy;
- amendment of the Founder Canon designation rules;
- transfer, sale or dissolution of WPA;
- transfer of core intellectual property, domains, repositories or trademarks;
- replacement of the primary institutional mission;
- abolition of the Human Gate;
- creation of autonomous AI institutional authority;
- removal or material rewriting of historical Founder attribution;
- merger or institutional combination that materially changes the identity of WPA.

For these matters, WPA should establish a **Founder Continuity Council** and an enhanced multi-human approval rule. The exact members and voting threshold must be formally appointed and should not be invented by AI.

---

## 9. Continuity asset map

Succession planning must cover, at minimum:

- WPA institutional identity and legal form;
- Institute structure and all sections;
- domain names and DNS control;
- website and hosting infrastructure;
- GitHub repositories and organisational access;
- Cloudflare Workers, routes, bindings and billing;
- institutional email accounts;
- Virtual Sande production and staging systems;
- WPAWS and agentic systems;
- Doctrine Kernel and canonical machine-readable policies;
- AI Council and candidate registries;
- Protocolometry;
- WPA Journal and editorial assets;
- books, papers, DOI/Zenodo records and publication archives;
- trademarks, logos and brand assets;
- contracts, licences and partner records;
- financial and payment accounts where applicable;
- archives, backups, cryptographic keys and recovery procedures;
- data-protection and privacy responsibilities;
- educational programmes, curricula and certification records where applicable.

Possession of credentials alone does not create authority.

> **Access is not mandate. Inheritance of credentials is not inheritance of institutional legitimacy.**

---

## 10. Successor access and credential transition

Credentials should be transferred through a controlled continuity procedure that separates:

1. legal/institutional authority;
2. identity verification;
3. technical access;
4. financial authority;
5. publication authority;
6. doctrine authority;
7. operational administration.

No single password, device, email account or recovery key should be treated as sufficient proof of institutional succession.

Critical systems should support documented recovery, multi-factor authentication and more than one trusted recovery path.

---

## 11. Founder Continuity Council

The Founder may appoint a small human council before succession activation to support the Primary Human Successor in constitutional matters.

Its purpose is protection, not displacement of the successor.

The Council should provide:

- continuity verification;
- independent review of HG4 constitutional changes;
- protection against coercion, fraud and credential takeover;
- confirmation of extraordinary transfers or dissolution;
- preservation of Founder Canon integrity;
- emergency continuity if the Primary Human Successor is temporarily unavailable.

Membership, alternates, quorum and voting thresholds require explicit Founder or later valid Human Authority appointment.

---

## 12. Backup succession

A backup human successor or succession mechanism should be formally named in a separate confidential schedule.

Virtual Sande may never become the backup successor.

If no valid human successor is available, consequential institutional action must fail closed, while archives and low-risk informational services may remain available in preservation mode.

---

## 13. Post-Founder publication integrity

Every post-Founder publication that uses Virtual Sande or Founder materials must distinguish among:

- direct Founder-authenticated quotation or doctrine;
- institutional interpretation;
- AI-assisted synthesis;
- new successor-authorised WPA doctrine.

Synthetic voice, avatar or likeness use must never create the false impression that the Founder personally approved a posthumous statement.

---

## 14. Emergency pause and preservation mode

Upon uncertainty about succession, compromise, conflicting claims or missing authority evidence, WPA systems must enter a **Preservation / Fail-Closed Mode** for consequential functions.

Allowed activities may include:

- read-only archives;
- public Founder Canon access;
- non-consequential educational retrieval;
- backups and security maintenance;
- incident response;
- preservation of evidence.

Blocked activities include new contracts, doctrine changes, financial commitments, credential issuance, institutional appointments and external commitments.

---

## 15. Non-transfer of human authority to AI

This constitutional rule is permanent unless replaced by a lawful human-governed constitutional act that still preserves final human authority:

> **A machine may preserve the pattern, memory and work of a person; it must not inherit the person’s human authority.**

> **Машината може да ги зачува образецот, меморијата и делото на човекот; не смее да ја наследи човечката власт на тој човек.**

## WPA Устав за континуитет на основачот и човечко наследување

Основачот Санде Смиљанов останува траен автор и основачки извор на Основачкиот канон.

**Вера-Патрициа Смиљанов** е назначена од Основачот како **примарен човечки институционален наследник на WPA**, со активирање по правно и институционално потврден настан на наследување и со нејзино прифаќање на улогата.

Virtual Sande може да продолжи да функционира по смртта или трајната неспособност на Основачот, но само во **режим на континуитет на Основачот**. Тој смее да го зачувува, пребарува, објаснува и применува постојното авторизирано знаење на WPA; не смее да се претставува како жив Санде Смиљанов, да измислува нови негови ставови, да ја наследи неговата лична власт или самостојно да создава институционални обврски.

По активирањето на наследувањето, **Вера-Патрициа Смиљанов станува живиот Human Authority**, а сите AI системи на WPA остануваат под Human Gate.

Основачкиот канон останува непроменлив историски и авторски запис. Подоцнежни коментари, дополнувања и институционални изданија мора јасно да бидат означени како пост-основачки.

Ниту лозинка, технички пристап, дигитален двојник, avatar, AI модел ниту наследена сесија не создаваат институционален мандат.

> **Пристапот не е мандат. Дигиталниот континуитет не е континуитет на човечката власт.**
