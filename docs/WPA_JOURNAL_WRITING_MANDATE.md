# WPA Journal Writing Mandate

Version: 0.1.0-draft  
Status: Draft policy for review  
Date: 2026-07-18

## Mission

WPA Journal is the central editorial, scientific and practitioner voice of the World Protocol Academy digital educational, research and author platform in development.

Its writing mission covers five core fields:

1. Protocol
2. Diplomacy and International Relations
3. Public Communication and Public Relations
4. Security Studies
5. Communicology and Strategic Communication

## Role separation

- WPA Watch detects public-source candidates.
- Journal Watch groups, ranks and converts candidates into editorial briefs.
- Diplomatic Analysis Lab develops structured interpretation.
- WPA Journal researches, writes and edits canonical long-form content.
- Social Studio adapts approved canonical content for platforms, languages and regions.
- The AI Editorial Council assists with research, comparison, drafting, localisation and risk detection.
- Sande Gate is the final human approval boundary.

## Permitted WPA Journal outputs

WPA Journal may prepare:

- editorials;
- daily and weekly analytical reviews;
- One Event — Five WPA Perspectives analyses;
- protocol and diplomatic signal reports;
- policy briefs;
- protocol notes;
- protocol scenario analyses;
- comparative institutional notes;
- analyses of protocol errors, state visits, summits and ceremonies;
- book and literature reviews;
- practitioner interviews;
- educational and methodology notes;
- regional and global dossiers;
- editorial responses to major public developments.

Every output must carry a clear type label, such as WPA Editorial, WPA Analysis, WPA Policy Brief, WPA Protocol Scenario, WPA Comparative Note, WPA Practitioner Insight or WPA Journal Explainer.

## Scientific publication boundary

No AI-generated or editorial draft may be represented as a peer-reviewed scientific article, accepted manuscript, official institutional position, legal advice, security advice, diplomatic instruction or position of the Ministry of Internal Affairs or another public body.

Scientific publication requires:

1. identifiable human authorship;
2. submission record;
3. desk review;
4. source and originality checks;
5. ethics, conflict-of-interest and AI-use declarations;
6. peer review where applicable;
7. revisions;
8. human editorial decision;
9. Sande Gate;
10. publication and correction record.

## Daily editorial task

The system may collect relevant public signals, classify them across the five fields, map sources, propose WPA angles, create an editorial brief and recommend one of these routes:

- journal analysis candidate;
- social draft candidate;
- student analysis task;
- research watch item;
- archive or rejection.

A daily brief is not a published article.

## Weekly editorial task

At least one verified topic may be developed into the signature format:

## One Event — Five WPA Perspectives

The event is analysed through protocol, diplomacy, public communication, security and communicology.

After human approval, the canonical Journal analysis may generate platform-native derivatives for LinkedIn, YouTube, podcast, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, Bluesky, Telegram, WhatsApp, Viber and other registered channels.

## Monthly editorial task

The Journal may prepare:

- one global thematic dossier;
- one regional review;
- one protocol or diplomatic signals digest;
- one methodology or practitioner note;
- one literature and source review;
- one future research agenda.

## AI Editorial Council

GPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Google, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, MiniMax–Mavis, Z.ai, Grok and Perplexity may assist with:

- source discovery and mapping;
- comparison of public evidence;
- research-angle proposals;
- preliminary summaries;
- draft structures and title options;
- language and regional adaptation;
- platform-format adaptation;
- bias, contradiction and risk detection;
- response drafting for later human review.

They may not:

- publish directly;
- impersonate a human author;
- invent sources, quotations or facts;
- make the final editorial decision;
- respond autonomously to high-risk political, legal, security, personal or crisis matters;
- process official-service, confidential, classified or unnecessary personal data.

Every AI contribution must record system, model or version, task, input provenance, output summary, human reviewer, corrections, decision and timestamp.

## Social distribution boundary

WPA Journal owns the canonical approved text. Social Studio may shorten, translate, visualise and adapt it, but may not change its factual meaning or editorial conclusion without renewed review.

Phase 2 proactive agents may prepare and queue responses across registered platforms and messengers. Automatic public replies remain disabled until official API access, account rights, platform-policy review, rate limits, moderation rules, audit logging and explicit activation approval are verified.

## Corrections and transparency

When an error is identified, WPA Journal must record the correction date, explain the material change, preserve version history where appropriate and withdraw content when integrity cannot be restored.

## Legal and commercial boundary

WPA is currently described as a digital educational, research and author platform in development, not as a registered university, public institution or legally established institute.

No prices, payments, publication fees, contracts, paid enrolment or commercial claims may be activated until a suitable legal, tax, accounting, privacy and payment framework is established and separately approved.

## Doctrine

WPA Watch sees. Journal Watch selects. Diplomatic Analysis Lab understands. WPA Journal researches and writes. Social Studio carries the approved text to the world. AI assists. Humans verify. Sande approves. WPA remains accountable for what it publishes.
