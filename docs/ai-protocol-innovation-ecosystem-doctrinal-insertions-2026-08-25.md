## 1. Governed Acceleration Principle

**Candidate rule:**

> AI may accelerate discovery, comparison, modelling, synthesis and iteration; it does not inherit institutional mandate, legitimacy or final responsibility.

**Macedonian:**

> Вештачката интелигенција може да ги забрза откривањето, споредбата, моделирањето, синтезата и итерацијата; таа не го наследува институционалниот мандат, легитимноста ниту конечната одговорност.

**Why it adds value:** This sharpens the existing Human Constitutional Primacy thesis by distinguishing *acceleration power* from *constitutional authority*.

**Natural placement:** chapters dealing with agentic systems, Human Gate, institutional AI deployment and final human responsibility.

**Relation to existing canon:**
- “Capability may become machine-superior; legitimacy, rights and final institutional responsibility must remain human.”
- “Access is not mandate.”
- “Content is not command.”

## 2. Capability–Resilience Pairing Principle

**Candidate rule:**

> Every new AI-enabled capability must be evaluated together with the dependency, vulnerability, misuse path and authority risk it creates.

**Macedonian:**

> Секоја нова способност овозможена од ВИ мора да се оценува заедно со зависноста, ранливоста, патеката на можна злоупотреба и ризикот за овластувањето што ги создава.

**Analytical test:**
1. What new capability is created?
2. What new dependency is introduced?
3. What can fail, drift or be misused?
4. Who can pause, override or revoke?
5. What evidence is required before release?

**Natural placement:** security, autonomous systems, embodied action, smart wearables, implants, critical infrastructure, quantum-era security and high-consequence AI.

**Possible aphorism:**

> Every new capability creates a new dependency.

**Macedonian:**

> Секоја нова способност создава нова зависност.

## 3. Institutional Feedback Legitimacy

**Candidate rule:**

> A legitimate AI institution must not only produce outputs; it must be able to observe outcomes, detect error, correct itself under human authority and return verified lessons to the knowledge cycle.

**Macedonian:**

> Легитимната институција што користи ВИ не смее само да произведува резултати; таа мора да може да ги набљудува исходите, да ја открива грешката, да се коригира под човечко овластување и проверените поуки повторно да ги внесува во циклусот на знаење.

**Proposed loop:**

Observe → Research → Verify → Model → Human Gate → Apply → Measure → Correct → Publish → Learn → Re-enter the cycle.

**Doctrinal significance:** This adds a temporal dimension to Human Gate. Human authority is required not only before consequential action, but also in the correction and institutional-learning loop after action.

**Natural placement:** governance architecture, institutional learning, adaptive systems, auditability and concluding operational doctrine.

## 4. Governed Connection Quality Principle

**Candidate rule:**

> Institutional strength is not the sum of its components; it is the quality of the governed connections between them.

**Macedonian:**

> Институционалната сила не е збир од нејзините компоненти; таа е квалитетот на управуваните врски меѓу нив.

**Why it matters for AI PROTOCOL:** The risk in complex AI ecosystems is often not inside one model, agent or database, but in the handoff between them: source → model, model → agent, agent → tool, tool → institution, institution → human decision.

**Derived control requirement:** Every consequential handoff should preserve mandate, provenance, scope, reversibility and accountable human ownership.

**Possible aphorism:**

> The handoff is part of the mandate.

**Macedonian:**

> Предавањето меѓу системите е дел од мандатот.

## 5. Discovery–Authority Separation, Extended

AI PROTOCOL already uses the logic that discovery and authority must remain distinct. The contemporary ecosystem model suggests extending this beyond therapeutic discovery.

**Candidate extension:**

> Discovery may be distributed across machines, institutions and networks; authority must remain explicitly located.

**Macedonian:**

> Откривањето може да биде распределено меѓу машини, институции и мрежи; овластувањето мора да остане јасно лоцирано.

**Natural placement:** general governance doctrine and the existing Discovery–Authority Separation Principle in the therapeutic/clinical legitimacy chapter as a cross-domain generalisation.

## 6. AI as Institutional Operating Layer — with Constitutional Limits

**Candidate formulation:**

> AI can become an operating layer across research, planning, simulation, administration and decision support without becoming the constitutional holder of authority.

**Macedonian:**

> Вештачката интелигенција може да стане оперативен слој низ истражувањето, планирањето, симулацијата, администрацијата и поддршката на одлучувањето, без да стане уставен носител на овластувањето.

**Doctrinal distinction:**
- operating layer ≠ sovereign layer
- coordination ≠ command
- recommendation ≠ authorisation
- system reach ≠ mandate

**Natural placement:** agentic institutions, liquid protocol, Human Authority and future institutional AI architectures.

## 7. Protocol of Consequential Handoffs

**Candidate new micro-protocol:** A high-consequence AI workflow should treat every transfer between systems as a governed event.

At each handoff record:
- source/provenance;
- current mandate;
- permitted scope;
- transformation performed;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- human owner;
- pause/override path;
- release condition.

**Candidate rule:**

> No consequential handoff without preserved provenance and visible authority.

**Macedonian:**

> Нема последично значајно предавање без зачувано доказно потекло и видливо овластување.

**Natural placement:** provenance, bounded agency, tool use, agent chains and institutional auditability.

## 8. Outcome-Based Legitimacy Metrics

A mature AI-governance regime should measure more than model accuracy or system uptime.

**Candidate institutional metrics:**
- time to detect consequential error;
- time to verified correction;
- verified-source coverage;
- human-review completion rate;
- repeat-error rate;
- mandate traceability;
- correction-loop time;
- decision-provenance completeness;
- rate of reversible vs irreversible automated actions;
- percentage of consequential actions passing Human Gate.

**Candidate rule:**

> Governance is credible only when its safeguards are measurable in operation.

**Macedonian:**

> Управувањето е веродостојно само кога неговите заштитни механизми се мерливи во практиката.

**Natural placement:** audit, protocol realism, institutional testing and concluding operational framework.

## 9. Convergence Risk Principle

When AI combines with robotics, sensors, quantum systems, biotechnology, implants, smart glasses or autonomous infrastructure, risk is not additive; interaction can create new categories of consequence.

**Candidate rule:**

> Converging capabilities require converging governance.

**Macedonian:**

> Конвергирачките способности бараат конвергирачко управување.

**Expanded formulation:** Governance architecture should follow the interaction surface between technologies, not the historical administrative boundaries between them.

**Natural placement:** future technology convergence, embodied AI, implants, human-machine interfaces, autonomous systems and post-quantum infrastructure.

## 10. Candidate synthesis box for AI PROTOCOL

### The Institutional AI Cycle

1. **Discover** — machines may search, compare and propose.
2. **Verify** — evidence and provenance are tested.
3. **Bound** — scope, mandate and agency are explicitly constrained.
4. **Authorise** — the Human Gate decides consequential release.
5. **Act** — execution remains within the authorised envelope.
6. **Measure** — outcomes and side effects are observed.
7. **Correct** — error, drift and unintended consequences trigger review.
8. **Learn** — verified lessons return to the institutional knowledge base.

**Closing rule:**

> The institution may learn continuously; its authority must remain continuously accountable.

**Macedonian:**

> Институцијата може континуирано да учи; нејзиното овластување мора континуирано да остане одговорно.
