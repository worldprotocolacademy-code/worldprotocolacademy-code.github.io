Academic Advisory Board (AAB)

AAB е независно советодавно тело во формирање. Неговата улога е методолошка критика, research-integrity review и советување; не создава државна акредитација, официјално признавање или управувачки мандат над WPA.

Public naming rule: No AAB member is named publicly before written acceptance, conflict-of-interest declaration and explicit public-listing consent.

Reviewer profiles sought

Protocol & diplomacy

Academic or senior practitioner perspective on protocol, diplomacy and institutional governance.

Methodology & research integrity

Independent review of evidence rules, scoring logic, correction and reproducibility.

AI governance / cybersecurity

Human authority, provenance, privacy, security and misuse boundaries.

Communication & public institutions

Institutional communication, reputation, ethics and public-interest perspective.

Acceptance sequence

  1. Private identification or expression of interest.
  2. Invitation with defined review scope and limits.
  3. Written acceptance of the advisory role.
  4. Conflict-of-interest declaration.
  5. Public Listing Consent and approved short biography.
  6. Only then: public naming on WPA surfaces.
  7. Material review findings enter a governance decision log; recommendations do not silently alter WPA doctrine.

Initial review priorities

Institute Index candidate

Review weights, evidence criteria, edge cases, worked examples and the measurement/accreditation boundary before v1.0.

Protocolometry

Review source discipline, repeatability, correction rules and case-study application.

Journal governance

Advisory input on flagship methodological or sensitive submissions where the published policy calls for it.

Human-governed AI

Evaluate provenance, mandate boundaries, capability-status transparency and correction/auditability.

Expression of interest

An expression of interest is not appointment, endorsement or partnership. Formal participation begins only after written acceptance and the applicable governance documents.
