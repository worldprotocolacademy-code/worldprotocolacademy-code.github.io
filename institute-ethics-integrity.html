Ethics & Integrity Code

Кодекс за јавната, истражувачката, аналитичката, образовната и AI-поддржаната работа на WPA Institute.

Core rule: institutional appearance must never be stronger than the evidence behind it. Capability, technology, design or ambition do not create authority, accreditation, partnership or verified fact.

1 · Truth & evidence

Distinguish verified fact, source-supported inference, interpretation, simulation, proposal and unresolved uncertainty.

2 · Institutional identity

Do not invent legal status, accreditation, physical offices, partnerships, boards, clients, endorsements or operational capacity.

3 · Human authority

AI may prepare, compare, classify or simulate. Consequential institutional responsibility and authorisation remain human.

4 · AI & provenance disclosure

Disclose AI-assisted or synthetic content when material; technology-specific provenance claims require actual evidence of deployment.

5 · Conflict of interest

Material conflicts are disclosed and managed. Recusal is required where impartial review cannot reasonably be maintained.

6 · No pay-to-rank

Payments, memberships, sponsorships, training or donations must never alter Index evidence, score, category or correction outcome.

7 · Privacy & minimisation

Collect only data needed for the declared purpose. Public forms must not solicit classified, restricted or unnecessary sensitive information.

8 · Dignity & fairness

No defamatory inference from missing data; no political retaliation, discriminatory treatment or unsupported reputational attack.

9 · Corrections & appeals

Material errors receive a visible correction path. A review body is never described as operational before it is actually constituted.

10 · Publication integrity

No invented ISSN, DOI, peer review, citation, reviewer or publication status. Labels must match the actual publication process.

11 · Commercial separation

Advisory and educational revenue is separated from analytical judgments, journal acceptance and correction rights.

12 · Real people & media

No fabricated testimonials, staged fake institutional life or synthetic founder/event imagery presented as documentary evidence.

Independent oversight status

WPA does not currently claim a separate independent oversight board. The Academic Advisory Board is in formation under its published acceptance and conflict-of-interest rules. Any future independent oversight structure will be named only after real people have formally accepted defined roles.
