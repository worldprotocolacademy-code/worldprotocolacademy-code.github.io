Institute Measurement & Ranking Strategy

The operational path from global institutional evidence to a validated WPA Protocolometry / Institute Index v1.0 — with ranking treated as an optional governed output, not the starting point.

Core rule: a calculable score is not permission to publish a ranking. Evidence quality, reproducibility, fairness, correction rights and human governance come first.

Strategic model

1 · Measure

Build source-traceable institutional dossiers and standard-referenced I01–I20 scorecards.

2 · Validate

Double-score, calibrate anchors, test weights, redundancy, missing-evidence behavior and peer-type fairness.

3 · Compare

Use peer diagnostics and a non-public Shadow Index only after the scoring model survives validation.

4 · Govern release

Publish only through an explicit human gate: scorecards, bands or ordinal ranking, depending on readiness.

Execution sequence

S0
Universe & entity resolution
Resolve identity, aliases, successors, branches and peer type before scoring.
S1
Evidence Dossier factory
Public evidence, provenance, institutional core and I01–I20 evidence status.
S2
Independent double scoring
Two separate candidate score vectors, disagreement log and human reconciliation.
S3
Calibration & fairness
Test small specialist, state, university-linked and international-training institutions for unintended structural bias.
S4
Robustness
Weight sensitivity, correlation/redundancy, evidence thresholds and rank-order stability diagnostics.
S5
Shadow Index
Non-public comparative output used only to detect unstable or unexplained results.
S6
Correction test
Institution-facing factual correction flow with source submission, decision log and version history.
S7
v1.0 freeze
Freeze definitions, weights, formulas, source rules, peer logic, snapshot date and changelog.
S8
Publication decision
Human governance chooses no release, scorecards, bands or ordinal ranking.

Release modes

R0 · Methodology only

No real-institution comparative publication.

R1 · Scorecards

Reviewed institutional scorecards and evidence coverage, without ordinal ordering.

R2 · Bands / profiles

Comparative bands or peer profiles without strict 1..N ordering.

R3 · Ordinal ranking

Possible only after all v1.0 readiness gates pass and a separate authorised governance decision.

Anti-gaming and fairness

Institution size, country power, fame, marketing reach and follower counts are not hidden quality proxies. Partnership quantity is not quality. Promotional claims do not become high scores without corroboration. The same achievement must not be double-counted across indicators. Non-English evidence must remain eligible. Missing public evidence remains NE unless sufficient evidence establishes verified non-conformance.
