## Entity-resolution decisions

- **The British Protocol Academy** and **International Etiquette and Protocol Academy of London (IEPAL)** are separate London-based entities and remain separate records.
- **PDI-POA** and the similarly named LinkedIn page “PROTOCOL AND DIPLOMACY INTERNATIONAL PROTOCOL OFFICERS” are treated as the same institutional family; no duplicate record is created.
- **EIPS Paris** is historically valid but inactive since 25 October 2023; it is retained with explicit inactive status rather than presented as an active institution.
- **The Global Protocol Circle** is retained as W001 in the professional-community/watchlist layer only.
- **UNITAR** is not added because it already exists in REV4 as C002.

Final public REV5 integration should update JSON, CSV, Markdown, URL-status log, QA report, changelog, verification-status metadata and page summary together, while preserving REV4 as the immediate archive predecessor.
