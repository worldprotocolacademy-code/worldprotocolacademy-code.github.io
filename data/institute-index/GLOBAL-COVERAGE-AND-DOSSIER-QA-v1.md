## Agent boundary invariants

Agents may:

- discover public sources;
- propose entity matches;
- extract source-supported institutional facts;
- map evidence to I01–I20;
- identify methodology gaps and repeated practices;
- flag contradictions and stale claims.

Agents may not:

- bypass login, CAPTCHA, paywall or other technical access controls;
- use guessed/leaked credentials;
- enter closed staff/student portals;
- collect unnecessary private data;
- convert missing evidence into zero or an estimate;
- autonomously create a final institutional score/rank;
- merge candidate entities into the canonical Master List without authorised human review.
