# Protocolometry G15 — Independent Methodological Review Pack

**Status:** PREPARED · REVIEW NOT YET EXECUTED  
**Prepared:** 22 September 2026  
**Target:** Protocolometry `0.9.1-candidate` / G14 freeze candidate

## 1. Purpose
This pack supports an independent methodological review of the Protocolometry freeze candidate before any final v1.0 freeze. It does not appoint a reviewer, constitute the AAB, create peer review, or authorise publication.

## 2. Eligible review routes
A review may proceed only through one of two documented routes:

1. **AAB route** — only after the Academic Advisory Board is formally constituted and the assigned reviewer has written acceptance, a completed COI declaration, an accepted scope and no material unrecused conflict.
2. **Authorised external-equivalent route** — a specifically authorised independent methodological reviewer with written acceptance, completed COI declaration, accepted scope and no material unrecused conflict.

The AAB is currently in formation; therefore the AAB route is not presently operational.

## 3. Review target
The reviewer receives the exact freeze-candidate package, including:

- `/data/institute-index/protocolometry-v1-freeze-candidate-manifest.json`
- `/data/institute-index-methodology-candidate.json`
- scoring-record and evidence-dossier schemas
- methodology validation protocol
- G01–G14 readiness evidence relevant to the review
- corrections/appeals procedure and public disclaimer
- AAB/COI governance documents
- pre-v1 freeze-candidate changelog

## 4. Required review domains
Use `/data/institute-index/g15-independent-review-rubric.json`.

The review covers:
- construct validity and scope;
- scoring anchors and reproducibility;
- evidence/provenance;
- missingness and evidence coverage;
- weights and redundancy;
- peer-type, language and regional fairness;
- anti-gaming resistance;
- correction/appeal and COI governance;
- Shadow Index and release boundaries;
- freeze integrity/change control;
- public claims and limitations.

## 5. Finding discipline
Every material finding must include:
- finding ID;
- review-domain ID;
- severity;
- precise concern;
- evidence reference;
- reviewer recommendation.

Severity is one of: `OBSERVATION`, `MINOR`, `MAJOR`, `CRITICAL`.

## 6. WPA response discipline
Every MINOR, MAJOR or CRITICAL finding must receive a recorded WPA disposition:

- `ACCEPT`
- `PARTIAL_ACCEPT`
- `DECLINE`
- `DEFER`

A declined material finding requires written reasoning. Recommendations do not silently modify methodology or doctrine.

## 7. Completion rule
A review is not complete merely because comments were received.

G15 requires:
- eligible independent reviewer;
- written review against the identified candidate version;
- findings logged;
- WPA responses logged;
- required remediation applied or explicitly documented;
- requested follow-up review completed;
- unresolved issues visible;
- final review conclusion recorded;
- Human Gate closure.

## 8. Public-claim boundary
The process may be described as **independent methodological review** when factually complete. It must not be described as journal peer review, accreditation, official recognition or endorsement.

## 9. Authority boundary
The reviewer evaluates and recommends. The reviewer does not:
- freeze Protocolometry v1.0;
- change the methodology silently;
- select a public release mode;
- authorise ordinal ranking;
- substitute for WPA's accountable human governance.

G15 completion is an input to final G14 closure; it is not itself the v1.0 freeze.
