Institute Charter

Six pillars of the Charter.

The Charter articulates the foundational principles under which the Institute operates — consistently, transparently and without special-case exceptions.

01

Academic integrity

All research and educational materials are original WPA creations or correctly attributed sources.

02

Professional measurability

Every programme, certificate and analytical indicator has clear criteria, transparent methodology and verifiable evidence.

03

Ethical independence

WPA does not permit payment for a better result, does not perform state accreditation and does not award state recognition.

04

Discipline of language

Terms such as partner, accredited or endorsed are not used without a separate written basis.

05

Transparent sources

Open methodology, structured citation practice and correction rights for institutions being measured.

06

Founder's doctrine

Protocol is treated as disciplined institutional order, responsibility and credible representation.

Analytics & Measurability

WPA Centre for Analytics, Measurability and Institutional Indices.

WPA is developing a specialised analytics centre to track, compare and evaluate institutions, academies, universities, programmes, experts and research activities across protocol, diplomacy, public communication, security studies and related fields.

What may be measured

Research output; verifiable citation visibility; programme and certification discipline; conferences and applied research; practical professional impact; international cooperation; public communication and digital transparency.

What the WPA Index is not

It is not state accreditation, a classical university ranking, a professional licence, a substitute for Scopus/Web of Science/ORCID/Google Scholar, or a pay-for-score system.
