WPA EDUCATION · CANONICAL QUALITY STANDARD

Programme Quality & Assessment Standard

Canonical framework for WPA professional non-degree programmes: learning outcomes, engagement, assessment, AI use, integrity, accessibility, appeals and future credential governance.

Status: IN DEVELOPMENT / PILOT. This standard governs programme design and pilot delivery. It does not activate official certificate issuance, degree status, professional licensing or automatic external recognition.

1. Required programme specification

ElementMinimum publication requirement
Learning outcomesObservable outcomes describing what a learner should know, explain, analyse or perform.

2. Assessment discipline

Before assessment

  • Outcomes and rubric fixed.
  • Weights/pass rules published.
  • Conflicts disclosed.
  • AI rules visible.

After assessment

  • Result traceable to rubric/version.
  • Feedback recorded.
  • Retake/appeal rights communicated.
  • No certificate claim unless issuance is active.

3. Question-bank quality

Question count is not by itself evidence of quality. Banks should document authorship, version, source basis, duplicate removal, difficulty balance, bias review, answer validation and retirement/revision rules.

4. AI and academic integrity

  • AI may support learning where permitted but does not replace learner responsibility.
  • Learners remain responsible for facts, citations and disclosed AI assistance.
  • Undisclosed substitution may be an integrity breach where prohibited by assessment rules.
  • AI output does not award, revoke or validate a credential.

5. Credential minimums

Official issuance requires holder identity verification, authorised issuing responsibility, serial/version control, completion evidence, correction/revocation, minimal public verification data and Human Gate approval.

6. Canonical level nomenclature

Foundation · Professional · Advanced · Trainer / Consultant. “Train-the-Trainer” may describe pedagogical focus, but Trainer / Consultant is the canonical public fourth-level pathway name.

7. External recognition

WPA does not claim automatic worldwide, state or academic recognition. Future certificates are independent professional records of completion and assessment; recognition remains a decision of each external institution, employer, regulator or jurisdiction.
