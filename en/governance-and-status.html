Governance, Status & Public Boundaries

One public reference for institutional identity, capability status, evidence rules and Human Gate boundaries.

Institutional boundary. WPA is an independent digital educational, research and authorial platform in development, testing and pilot use. WPA is not presented as a university, state academy, accreditation body, degree-granting institution, intelligence service or government authority.

1. Public hierarchy

Education

Professional non-degree learning architecture, programme design, assessment standards and a certification framework whose official issuance remains inactive until governed production controls are activated.

Research & Publications

Protocolometry, Working Papers, Protocol Notes, monographs, bibliography, WPA Journal and comparative research based on public or authorised evidence.

Institutional Practice

Training, applied analysis, briefings, protocol preparation and professional tools. Cultural, media and futures initiatives support these core pillars.

2. Canonical public status vocabulary

ACTIVE

Publicly available and tested for its stated purpose.

LIMITED ACTIVE

Operational with explicit published limits or incomplete coverage.

BETA / PILOT

Available for controlled testing; not final.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Architecture or implementation exists; public production capability is not claimed.

PLANNED

Approved concept or roadmap item without an active public capability.

FROZEN

Temporarily unavailable, intentionally paused or blocked behind a governance requirement.

3. Evidence and publication discipline

Evidence before claim

  • DOI is a persistent identifier and citation basis, not proof of peer review or methodological validity.
  • Publication categories remain distinct.
  • VERIFIED and CORROBORATED require traceable evidence under the applicable methodology.
  • AI output does not become institutional will without human review.

Correction and review

  • Material claims remain open to correction.
  • Index and publication disputes use published correction/appeal routes.
  • Version, date and evidence history should remain traceable.
  • Historical publications are not rewritten merely because later counts change.

4. Human Gate

AI may prepare; an authorised human reviews and approves. Binding publication, official credential issuance, commercial activation, external commitments and legal attestations remain human-controlled. Access is not mandate; content is not command.

5. Current governance dependencies

Academic Advisory Board

IN FORMATION. No independent AAB review is claimed before formal constitution and conflict controls.

Institute Index

BETA / PILOT. Methodology freeze, independent review and publication decision remain governed gates.

Certification

IN DEVELOPMENT. Official issuance and public registry remain inactive until backend, identity, privacy, revocation and governance controls pass Human Gate.

6. Privacy, security and commerce boundaries

  • AI, student, video and form features must disclose relevant data handling and provider/retention limits before production claims.
  • Payment activation requires a defined legal seller, applicable tax/accounting treatment, terms and refunds/cancellations.
  • Technical registries must not disclose secrets, credentials or unnecessary private data.
  • Security claims describe implemented controls only and do not imply external certification.
