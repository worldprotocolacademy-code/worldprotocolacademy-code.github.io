# WPA Marketing Operating System v1.0

**Status:** Active operational strategy  
**Owner:** World Protocol Academy (WPA)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** 2026-08-25

## 1. Strategic objective

Build a repeatable global marketing system that converts WPA's existing intellectual property, professional expertise, public research, references and institutional services into discoverability, trust, owned audience, qualified enquiries and long-term institutional relationships.

The system is not designed around vanity follower growth. It is designed around a measurable sequence:

**POSITION -> CREATE -> HOOK -> TEACH -> ENGAGE -> MEASURE -> FIND WINNER -> MULTIPLY -> CAPTURE -> CONVERT -> RETARGET -> SCALE**

## 2. Core value translation

WPA does not market file formats, page counts or generic content. It translates each offer into the professional result it creates.

| Offer | Avoid leading with | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Protocol / symbols reference | PDF or page count | certainty and readiness before an official event |
| Publications | number of pages | structured professional knowledge and authority |
| Training | lessons / modules | professional readiness and institutional capacity |
| AI-related doctrine | another AI publication | human authority, legitimacy and responsibility in AI-assisted institutions |
| Institutional services | consulting hours | reduced protocol risk, stronger representation and decision readiness |

## 5. Content architecture

### Six permanent content pillars

1. **Protocol in 30 Seconds** — one useful rule or professional distinction.
2. **Spot the Protocol Mistake** — discovery, interaction and visual learning.
3. **Diplomatic Scenario** — decision situations and professional judgement.
4. **Symbols & Ceremonies** — flags, anthems, national days, ceremonial sequencing.
5. **AI Protocol / Institutional AI Governance** — human authority, mandate, responsibility and bounded agency.
6. **Inside WPA / Founder Notes** — research process, professional judgement, institutional discipline and selected founder perspective.

### Three functional layers

Initial mix for the first 30–50 pieces of short-form content:

- **50% Discovery** — earns attention from people who do not yet know WPA.
- **35% Authority** — proves professional depth and source discipline.
- **15% Conversion** — gives a qualified next step.

These percentages are starting hypotheses. They must be adjusted from performance data.

## 6. Video architecture

Default short-form structure:

- **0–3 sec — Hook:** question, error, tension, surprising professional distinction.
- **3–15 sec — Problem:** explain what is at stake.
- **15–35 sec — Value:** teach one clear rule or framework.
- **35–50 sec — Payoff:** resolve the hook and explain why it matters.
- **Final seconds — CTA:** one action only.

CTA hierarchy:

1. **Value CTA:** Save this / follow for professional protocol rules.
2. **Community CTA:** What would you do? / Send your protocol question.
3. **Owned-audience CTA:** Start at worldprotocolacademy.mk/start/.
4. **Qualified commercial CTA:** Institutional proposal / reference / programme only when context warrants it.

## 8. WPA Winner Loop

Every 10 published pieces, review performance and produce a written learning note.

### Measure
- first-seconds retention where available
- average watch time
- completion rate
- saves
- shares
- meaningful comments
- profile visits
- follows
- visits to `/start/`
- resource usage / reference clicks
- qualified institutional enquiries

### Winner definition
A **WPA Winner** materially exceeds the recent WPA median in at least two metrics relevant to its purpose.

Examples:
- Discovery winner: retention + shares.
- Utility winner: saves + completion.
- Authority winner: completion + profile visits / follows.
- Conversion winner: landing-page visits + qualified enquiries.

### Winner action
Do not immediately abandon a winner for a new topic. Generate 5–10 controlled extensions around the winning subject, hook or format.

## 9. Ten-video experiment rule

Improve one principal variable at a time so learning remains interpretable.

- Videos 1–10: **Hook Lab**
- 11–20: **Retention / structure Lab**
- 21–30: **Save & Share Lab**
- 31–40: **CTA Lab**
- 41–50: **Conversion Lab**

Do not simultaneously redesign hook, set, duration, CTA and tone and then claim to know what caused the result.

## 10. Platform roles

### TikTok
Discovery, native human delivery, strong hooks, comments, creator-style utility.

### Instagram
Premium visual treatment, Reels, carousels, stories, social proof and community.

### YouTube Shorts
Evergreen searchable short-form knowledge; titles should describe the actual professional question.

### LinkedIn
B2B / institutional authority, document posts, explainers, research summaries, professional scenarios, founder insight and institutional calls to action.

### Facebook
Adapted educational video, community distribution and institutional / professional audience reach.

One master idea may be reused across platforms, but the final asset must be platform-native rather than a blind copy-paste.

## 11. Language policy

- Global primary language: **English**.
- Macedonian and other regional / target-market versions are adaptations, not mandatory duplicate subtitle layers on every video.
- Localise proven winners first.
- Expansion languages should follow target-market evidence, institutional strategy and available quality control.

## 12. Production system

Use batch production rather than reinventing a video each day.

Minimum recurring production kit:
- high-quality phone or camera
- reliable microphone
- clean WPA-branded or neutral professional environment
- consistent subtitle and hook typography
- subtle WPA identity mark
- master export without third-party platform watermark

Flagship films may use premium production. Daily organic content should remain sustainable and human.

## 13. Social-to-WPA funnel

**Social discovery -> Profile -> /start/ -> Quick Check / Reference / Institutional Services**

Current primary pathways:

- Free utility: `/resources/official-event-quick-check/`
- Protocol & symbols environment: `/protocol-symbols.html`

Do not force every viewer directly into a sale. Match the next step to audience intent.

## 14. UTM convention

When links can carry analytics parameters, use a stable naming convention:

- `utm_source=tiktok|instagram|youtube|linkedin|facebook`
- `utm_medium=organic-social` (or `paid-social` when applicable)
- `utm_campaign=wpa-social-launch-2026`
- `utm_content=<pillar>-<asset-id>`

Example:

`/start/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=wpa-social-launch-2026&utm_content=planb-carousel-01`

Do not encode personal or sensitive information in URLs.

## 15. Paid amplification policy

Organic proof comes first. Paid media should normally amplify content, hooks or offers that have already demonstrated useful organic signals.

Do not use spend to disguise weak positioning or weak creative.

## 16. Creator / ambassador system

Every external creator relationship must define:
- deliverables
- platforms
- organic usage rights
- paid-media usage rights
- term
- territory
- exclusivity if any
- review / revision scope
- factual approval workflow
- disclosure obligations
- delivery dates
- tracking method
- payment terms
- reporting access

Creator evaluation must consider audience fit, geography, authority, engagement quality, content quality, brand safety, platform strength, conversion potential and rights cost — not follower count alone.

## 17. Response engine

Every meaningful response channel has a route:

- useful public question -> answer and candidate video topic
- product / reference question -> relevant public page
- institutional need -> proposal route
- creator / ambassador interest -> partnership review
- correction / evidence challenge -> source review and correction workflow

Comments are both community and market research. They must be reviewed systematically.

## 18. Commercial calendar

Build content and outreach before relevant buying / planning windows:
- academic-year planning
- diplomatic / protocol event cycles
- conference seasons
- institutional training budgets
- Q4 procurement / planning
- publication launches
- relevant AI / governance events

The content engine should create awareness before the decision window begins.

## 19. Success standard

The first 30–50 pieces are not judged primarily by virality. Their strategic output is **WPA first-party market intelligence**: which problems, hooks, formats and offers cause the right professional audience to stop, save, return, inquire and trust.
