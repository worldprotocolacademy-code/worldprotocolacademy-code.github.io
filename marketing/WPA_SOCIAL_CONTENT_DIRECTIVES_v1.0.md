# WPA Social Content Directives v1.0

**Status:** Mandatory operating directives  
**Applies to:** WPA-owned social media, founder content published under the WPA strategy, contractors, agencies, creators and ambassadors producing WPA-related content  
**Effective:** 2026-08-25

## Directive 1 — Accuracy outranks virality

No hook, caption, carousel, video or advertisement may trade factual accuracy for dramatic effect.

Before publication, factual claims must be traceable to one of the following:
1. an official / competent authority for time-sensitive facts;
2. a canonical WPA publication or approved WPA source;
3. a clearly identified external authoritative source.

If a fact is uncertain, the content is held until verified.

## Directive 2 — Never invent publication status

Never state or imply that a concept, paper, protocol note, index, DOI, certification, award or institutional relationship exists unless the canonical record has been verified.

Specifically:
- do not invent DOI strings or use “DOI-LOCKED” as a decorative credibility claim;
- do not describe a draft as a published WPA Working Paper or Protocol Note;
- do not attribute a coined index to WPA unless the source document actually introduces it;
- do not attribute a quotation to Assoc. Prof. Dr. Sande Smiljanov unless the wording is verified in a canonical source.

Unpublished ideas must be marked **Concept**, **Discussion Draft** or **Internal Draft — Not for Publication** as appropriate.

## Directive 3 — Time-sensitive information requires current verification

Flags, office-holders, titles, national days, diplomatic status, official visits, precedence rules and other changeable institutional facts must be verified before use at an official event and before publication when currency is material.

Public-facing content should distinguish evergreen principles from time-sensitive facts.

## Directive 4 — WPA tone

WPA content must be:
- calm;
- precise;
- authoritative;
- useful;
- human;
- internationally understandable.

Avoid:
- cheap sensationalism;
- empty superlatives;
- generic motivational posting unrelated to WPA domains;
- pompous institutional language where a simple sentence works better;
- clickbait without a clear educational payoff.

A dramatic hook is acceptable only when the body earns it.

## Directive 5 — Founder positioning

Founder content should strengthen professional and intellectual authority, not imitate generic influencer culture.

Suitable founder themes include:
- professional judgement;
- authority and responsibility;
- dignity and representation;
- institutional conduct;
- protocol realism;
- leadership under pressure;
- human authority in AI-assisted systems;
- research and publication process.

## Directive 6 — One asset, one primary job

Every planned asset must be labelled before production as one of:
- **Discovery**;
- **Authority**;
- **Conversion**.

A piece may generate secondary benefits, but the primary job determines its hook, structure and KPI.

## Directive 7 — One primary CTA

Do not overload content with multiple competing calls to action.

Choose one principal action:
- save;
- share;
- comment;
- follow;
- visit `/start/`;
- open a relevant reference;
- submit an institutional enquiry.

## Directive 8 — Short-form structure

Default structure for TikTok, Reels and Shorts:

**Hook -> Problem -> Value -> Payoff -> CTA**

The first seconds must earn attention without misleading the viewer.

Avoid long animated logos, ceremonial introductions or title sequences before the hook.

## Directive 9 — Platform-native adaptation

Do not blindly repost the same rendered asset everywhere.

Maintain one clean master source and adapt:
- hook pacing;
- caption length;
- title / metadata;
- cover treatment;
- CTA;
- aspect and safe-zone treatment;
- professional context.

Do not upload third-party-platform watermarks when a clean master exists.

## Directive 10 — Visual identity

Default WPA social visual system:
- dark base: `#0f172a` or the approved current WPA dark palette;
- WPA gold: `#D4AF37`, with `#C5A059` available as a secondary institutional gold;
- restrained, high-contrast typography;
- consistent subtitle treatment;
- small, non-obstructive WPA identity mark;
- generous whitespace;
- no decorative clutter that competes with the professional point.

A social asset should become recognisably WPA before the viewer reads the account name.

## Directive 11 — Carousels / document posts

Default educational document-post sequence:
1. Cover / hook.
2. Professional tension or problem.
3. WPA framework or distinction.
4. Example / evidence / application.
5. Takeaway.
6. CTA / source where needed.

Five or six pages are preferred when the idea can be expressed clearly at that length; more pages are allowed when the content genuinely requires them.

Do not claim that a particular pixel size, PDF format or first-comment link tactic is an algorithmic guarantee. Treat presentation and link placement as test variables and measure actual results.

## Directive 12 — Source presentation

Where a social asset is based on a WPA paper, protocol note or handbook:
- verify the exact title;
- verify the identifier;
- verify DOI / repository URL if claimed;
- use wording faithful to the source;
- distinguish source wording from new editorial interpretation;
- include a source line on the final slide or in the caption when appropriate.

## Directive 13 — Comments are an editorial input

Meaningful comments must be reviewed for:
- recurring questions;
- misunderstandings;
- geographic demand;
- potential new content;
- correction requests;
- professional or institutional leads.

A strong question should be considered for a reply-video or future carousel.

## Directive 14 — Winner replication

When a piece materially outperforms the WPA recent median in relevant metrics:
- identify why it likely won;
- preserve the winning variable where possible;
- generate 5–10 adjacent tests;
- avoid cloning superficial details without understanding the audience need.

## Directive 15 — Ten-video controlled improvement

During an experiment batch, nominate one main variable to improve. Keep other major variables reasonably stable.

Document the hypothesis before the batch and the learning after it.

## Directive 16 — Commercial integrity

WPA marketing translates real value; it does not manufacture false scarcity, fake credentials, fictitious demand, fabricated testimonials or misleading institutional status.

Premium positioning must make genuine value visible, not invent value that does not exist.

## Directive 17 — Institutional boundaries

Never imply that WPA:
- represents a state;
- grants diplomatic recognition;
- provides government certification unless formally authorised;
- replaces competent official authorities;
- provides legal advice when it is not acting in that capacity.

Use the existing public boundary and disclaimer framework consistently.

## Directive 18 — Creator / ambassador briefing

Before recording, external creators must receive an approved brief containing:
- objective;
- audience;
- core claim(s);
- source pack;
- prohibited / unverified claims;
- required disclosure if applicable;
- CTA;
- brand assets;
- deliverables;
- revision allowance.

Fact checking should happen before expensive reshoots whenever possible.

## Directive 19 — Rights and commercial terms

Creator agreements must separate, where relevant:
- production fee;
- creator-channel publication;
- organic brand usage rights;
- paid advertising rights;
- platform scope;
- duration;
- geography;
- exclusivity;
- revisions.

Never assume a delivered video grants unlimited reuse rights.

## Directive 20 — Pre-publication gate

Every WPA social asset must pass this minimum gate:

**A. Scope** — Is it clearly relevant to WPA?  
**B. Value** — Does the audience learn, decide or understand something useful?  
**C. Evidence** — Are factual and attribution claims verified?  
**D. Brand** — Does it strengthen the intended WPA positioning?  
**E. Rights** — Are visual, music, creator and source rights appropriate?  
**F. Platform** — Is the final version native to the channel?  
**G. CTA** — Is there one clear next action?  
**H. Tracking** — Is the asset ID / campaign naming captured where relevant?

If any mandatory item fails, the asset is not published.

## Directive 21 — Incident / correction rule

If WPA discovers a material public error:
1. stop scheduled reuse of the affected asset;
2. verify the correction;
3. correct or remove the inaccurate version where feasible;
4. publish a clarification when the error could materially mislead;
5. record the cause so the content process improves.

## Directive 22 — Monthly operating review

At least once per month, review:
- top winners and why they won;
- weakest assets and why;
- most saved / shared topics;
- questions repeatedly asked;
- traffic to `/start/`;
- institutional enquiries attributable to social;
- content gaps;
- upcoming calendar opportunities;
- claims or sources needing refresh.

This directive set is subordinate to applicable law, platform rules, copyright, privacy obligations and WPA's public disclaimer / correction framework.
