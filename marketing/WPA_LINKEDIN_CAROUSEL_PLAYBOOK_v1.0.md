# WPA LinkedIn Carousel Playbook v1.0

**Status:** Operational draft and publication gate  
**Effective:** 2026-08-25

This document reviews the four proposed LinkedIn PDF/document carousel concepts and converts them into publishable WPA standards. It preserves strong hooks and practical value while removing unsupported algorithm claims, unverifiable attribution and premature publication-status claims.

## General LinkedIn document-post rule

Use a concise sequence: **Hook -> Problem -> WPA framework -> Application -> Takeaway -> CTA / source**.

Recommended design direction:
- portrait-first layout where practical (for example 1080 × 1350) or square when the concept genuinely benefits from it;
- WPA dark base (`#0f172a`) with WPA gold (`#D4AF37`, secondary `#C5A059`);
- one dominant idea per page;
- readable typography at mobile size;
- source / DOI line on the final page when a canonical publication is being cited.

Important: do **not** present “LinkedIn penalises external links in the post” as a verified platform rule. Link placement is a test variable. Measure post-link, first-comment and profile / landing-page routes rather than treating one tactic as universal algorithm doctrine.

# Carousel 2 — Plan B in Protocol / WP-002

**Publication status:** GREEN WITH EDITS, subject to exact source wording and DOI check.

## Why it works
- high practical B2B value;
- natural bridge to institutional training / protocol audit;
- easy to save and share;
- converts a research paper into an operational concept.

## Required corrections

1. Preserve the paper's actual distinction between contingency and improvisation.
2. Avoid absolute operational details not established by the paper (for example, a universal “24 hours before” technical-test rule) unless separately sourced and framed as WPA operational guidance.
3. Avoid implying that one fallback model applies identically to state visits, summits and corporate conferences.
4. Institutional Protocol Audit is a supported WPA service and may be used as the service CTA.

## Recommended 6-page structure

### Page 1 — Cover
**PLAN B IN PROTOCOL**  
*When the perfect programme meets reality.*

### Page 2 — Reality
**Official events are designed. Reality is variable.**

Weather, security, health, travel disruption and technical failure can alter the programme. Professional protocol anticipates disruption before the room opens.

### Page 3 — WPA distinction
**Contingency is not improvisation.**

Use source-faithful wording: a prepared alternative protects continuity, dignity and institutional order when the primary plan cannot proceed.

### Page 4 — Three planning questions
1. **Alternative logistics:** Is there a viable fallback location, route or sequence?
2. **Role continuity:** Who assumes a function if a principal participant or operational lead changes?
3. **Communication:** Who issues the verified message and through which channel?

### Page 5 — Operational takeaway
**A Plan B should preserve purpose — not merely keep the event moving.**

The fallback must still respect the institutional objective, roles, dignity and safety boundaries of the event.

### Page 6 — Source / CTA
**Does your institution have a written contingency protocol?**

Source: *WPA Working Paper 002* — exact title + verified DOI.

CTA: Institutional Protocol Audit / full paper.

# Carousel 3 — “Neuroprotocol 2030 / EECI / Right to Pause”

**Publication status:** RED / HOLD.

## Reason for hold

At the time of this review, the proposed public identifiers and attributions — including **WPA-PN-006**, **Neuroprotocol 2030**, **Effective Embodied Command Index (EECI)**, **Right to Pause**, and the statement **DOI-LOCKED** — were not sufficiently verified against a canonical public WPA record.

They must not be presented as an already published WPA Protocol Note or DOI-backed framework until the source exists and has been checked.

## Safe options

### Option A — Hold until canonical publication
Create the underlying paper / note, complete editorial and bibliographic review, register / publish it, then build the carousel from the canonical text.

### Option B — Reframe as an explicitly labelled discussion concept
Possible working title:

**HUMAN AUTHORITY IN AI-ASSISTED PROTOCOL**  
*What should never be delegated without a human gate?*

This version must not use unverified index names or publication identifiers. It may use already canonical WPA doctrine only where the wording is verifiably published / approved.

## Publication gate

Do not release until each of the following is verified:
- exact document title;
- exact identifier;
- publication status;
- canonical URL / DOI where claimed;
- exact definitions of any coined index;
- attribution of “Right to Pause” or equivalent doctrine.

# Carousel 4 — Five Rules for a Professional Conference

**Publication status:** AMBER / REWORK BEFORE PUBLICATION.

## Why it works
- broad professional relevance;
- high save / share potential;
- accessible entry point into WPA expertise;
- strong bridge to the new WPA Official Event Quick Check.

## Main issue

The proposed version attributes several strong propositions to a “2020 handbook” but does not provide the exact canonical title / page basis in the draft. Several lines read as absolute doctrine rather than sourced guidance.

Examples needing source verification or reframing:
- “The protocol map dictates the budget.”
- “Technical gaffe = protocol gaffe.”
- universal technical testing exactly 24 hours beforehand;
- “Time is absolute.”
- “Documentation is eternal.”

These may be effective editorial hooks, but they must not be presented as direct WPA handbook doctrine unless supported.

## Recommended safer 6-page version

### Page 1 — Cover
**5 CHECKS BEFORE A PROFESSIONAL CONFERENCE OPENS**

*The details guests notice are often decided long before arrival.*

### Page 2 — 1. Map the protocol before the room
Confirm participants, roles, precedence, seating, symbols, speaking order and official movements before locking logistical decisions that depend on them.

### Page 3 — 2. Treat technical continuity as event continuity
Microphones, presentation systems, interpretation, lighting and connectivity require tested fallback arrangements appropriate to the event.

### Page 4 — 3. Design the official image
Backgrounds, lecterns, flags, camera lines and lighting shape how the institution and participants will be represented publicly.

### Page 5 — 4. Protect timing / 5. Close the record
Respect agreed timing and maintain an appropriate event record, decisions, final programme and follow-up documentation according to institutional requirements.

### Page 6 — CTA
**Before your next official event, run the 12-point WPA Quick Check.**

CTA: `worldprotocolacademy.mk/start/` or `/resources/official-event-quick-check/`.

If the 2020 handbook is used as the source, add the exact title and bibliographic reference after verification.

# Publication workflow for every WPA carousel

1. **Idea ID** — assign a stable content ID.
2. **Purpose** — Discovery, Authority or Conversion.
3. **Source pack** — canonical WPA / official / authoritative source.
4. **Copy draft** — one main idea per page.
5. **Evidence review** — every factual / attribution claim checked.
6. **Design** — WPA visual system; mobile readability.
7. **Rights review** — imagery, logos, photos, music if used in video derivative.
8. **CTA + UTM** — one primary CTA; stable campaign naming.
9. **Publish** — native LinkedIn document post.
10. **Measure** — impressions, dwell / document engagement where available, reactions, comments, saves, shares, profile visits, landing-page traffic and qualified enquiries.
11. **Winner loop** — successful topic becomes a controlled series, not a one-off.

# Recommended release order

1. **Plan B in Protocol** — broad practical B2B value.
2. **Vatican Protocol** — distinctive authority / research content.
3. **Five Checks Before a Professional Conference Opens** — broad utility / funnel to Quick Check.
4. **AI / Neuroprotocol concept** — only after publication-status verification or explicit concept reframing.
