Source discipline & ethics

  • All products are based on author-reviewed WPA Working Papers and public Zenodo DOI records where applicable.
  • Briefings use public-source analysis only. No classified, private, confidential, or intelligence collection is requested or processed.
  • Paid briefings are separate from WPA Journal editorial decisions, peer review, publication fees, indexing, certificates and institutional benchmarks.
  • Client context can guide emphasis, but it cannot buy a predetermined finding, ranking, endorsement, or academic conclusion.
  • For sensitive state, territorial or diplomatic matters, wording remains neutral, source-disciplined and human-reviewed.
