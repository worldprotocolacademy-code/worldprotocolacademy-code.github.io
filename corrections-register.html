Материјалните корекции на јавните WPA тврдења се евидентираат така што посетителот може да ја разбере тековната канонска состојба. Историски публикации не се препишуваат само затоа што корпусот подоцна се променил.

2026-11-12 · Publication metrics

Academic and Zenodo counts separated

Canonical public wording: 27 academic publications = 6 monographs/handbooks + 1 doctoral dissertation + 20 scientific papers/contributions. Separate DOI corpus: 25 Zenodo records = 15 Working Papers + 9 Protocol Notes + 1 Global Strategic Plan. The two totals are not added together.

2026-11-12 · Certification

Inactive issuance made explicit

Public FAQ and certification language clarify that official WPA certificate issuance and public registry are not currently active; future certificates do not imply automatic state, academic or worldwide recognition.

2026-11-12 · Governance

AAB and capability statuses clarified

The Academic Advisory Board is explicitly in formation. Trust Layer and WPAWS distinguish an active core/working version from functions still in development.

2026-11-12 · Institutional boundary

Supplementary-activity consent clarified

The published wording now states that the founder's supplementary-activity consent is not state accreditation, legal registration, institutional endorsement or official recognition of WPA or WPA Institute.

2026-11-12 · Methodology

Authorial maxim separated from measurement proof

“Протоколот е апсолутен.” remains an authorial maxim. Public methodology now requires explicit criteria, verifiable evidence, limitations, fairness controls, corrections and independent-review gates rather than inferring measurability from the maxim.

Correction route

Submit a material factual, metadata, rights or methodology correction through Correction Request. Journal-specific corrections and retractions follow the Journal policy; Institute Index disputes use Index Appeals & Corrections.
