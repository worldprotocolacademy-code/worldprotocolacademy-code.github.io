WPA Scenario Film Lab

Развојна WPA/Institute програмска линија за претворање протоколарни случаи и истражувачки наоди во сценарија, симулации, аудио-визуелни лекции и образовни материјали.

Institutional boundary: WPA Scenario Film Lab is an educational and research-development programme line; it is not presented as a commercial film production company. Production, partnership, advisory or reviewer roles are stated only after formal acceptance and agreement.

Learning pipeline

1 · Case

Documented protocol case, incident or teaching question.

2 · Scenario

Educational script with facts, assumptions and simulation clearly separated.

3 · Audio / video

Learning media or simulation with provenance and AI/synthetic-content disclosure where applicable.

4 · Lesson

Teaching notes, protocol analysis, correction points and human-reviewed takeaways.

Governance rules

  • Documentary evidence and fictional/simulated elements must not be conflated.
  • AI-assisted or synthetic media should be visibly disclosed according to the WPA Trust Layer.
  • Rights, attribution, consent and institutional dignity are checked before publication.
  • No external organisation or person is described as partner, producer, adviser or reviewer without formal acceptance and agreement.
  • A scenario does not create an official account of an event merely because it is realistic.
