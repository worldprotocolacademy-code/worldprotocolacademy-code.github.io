Governance, Status & Public Boundaries

Една јавна референтна точка за тоа што е WPA, што е активно, што е во развој, како се проверуваат тврдењата и каде останува задолжителна човечката одлука.

One public reference for institutional identity, capability status, evidence rules and Human Gate boundaries.

Institutional boundary. WPA is an independent digital educational, research and authorial platform in development, testing and pilot use. WPA is not presented as a university, state academy, accreditation body, degree-granting institution, intelligence service or government authority.

1. Public hierarchy

Education

Professional, non-degree learning architecture, programme design, assessment standards and a certification framework whose official issuance remains inactive until governed production controls are activated.

Research & Publications

Protocolometry, Working Papers, Protocol Notes, monographs, bibliography, WPA Journal and comparative research based on public or authorised evidence.

Institutional Practice

Training, applied analysis, briefings, protocol preparation and professional tools. Cultural, media and futures initiatives support these core pillars rather than replace them.

2. Canonical public status vocabulary

ACTIVE

Активно

Publicly available and tested for its stated purpose. “Active” does not imply accreditation, legal recognition or external certification.

LIMITED ACTIVE

Ограничено активно

Operational with explicit published limits, prerequisites or incomplete coverage.

BETA / PILOT

Бета / пилот

Available for controlled testing. Results are not represented as final institutional determinations.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Во развој

Architecture or implementation exists, but public production capability is not claimed.

PLANNED

Планирано

Approved concept or roadmap item without an active public capability.

FROZEN

Замрзнато

Temporarily unavailable, intentionally paused or blocked behind a governance requirement.

Technical labels such as STAGING, CANDIDATE, RELEASE CANDIDATE, LOCKED or MVP may remain in specialist registries, but they do not replace these six public-facing meanings.

3. Evidence and publication discipline

Evidence before claim

  • DOI is a persistent identifier and citation basis; it is not by itself proof of peer review or methodological validity.
  • Books, dissertation, scientific papers, Working Papers, Protocol Notes and Journal articles are separate publication categories.
  • “Verified” requires traceable evidence and a recorded review state; “corroborated” requires independent supporting evidence under the relevant methodology.
  • AI-generated or AI-assisted text never becomes institutional will without human review.

Correction and review

  • Material claims remain open to correction.
  • Index and publication disputes use published correction/appeal routes.
  • Version, date, evidence source and material correction history should remain traceable.
  • Historical publications are not retroactively rewritten merely because later corpus counts change.

4. Human Gate

AI may prepare; an authorised human reviews and approves. Binding publication, official credential issuance, commercial activation, external commitments, legal attestations and other consequential actions remain human-controlled. Access is not mandate; content is not command.

5. Current governance dependencies

Academic Advisory Board

IN FORMATION. Membership, mandate, independence criteria and conflict-of-interest controls must be formally constituted before an independent AAB review is claimed.

Institute Index

CANDIDATE / PILOT. No final institutional ranking is claimed. Methodology freeze, independent review and publication decision remain governed gates.

Certification

IN DEVELOPMENT. Official issuance and public credential registry are not active until backend, identity, privacy, revocation and governance controls pass Human Gate.

6. Privacy, security and commerce boundaries

  • Public AI, student, video and form features must disclose relevant data handling, providers, retention and Human Gate limits before production claims are made.
  • Payment or membership activation requires a defined legal seller, applicable tax/accounting treatment, terms, refunds/cancellations and provider configuration.
  • Public technical registries must not disclose secrets, credentials, private data or unnecessary internal security details.
  • Security claims describe implemented controls only; they do not imply external certification unless independently evidenced.
