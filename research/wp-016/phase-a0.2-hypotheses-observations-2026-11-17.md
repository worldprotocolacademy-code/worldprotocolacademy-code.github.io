## Hypotheses fixed in advance

**H1 - Host-axis hypothesis (APEC comparative photograph).** In an authenticated APEC family photograph showing Xi, Trump and Putin, the host Xi is predicted to occupy the central protocol axis of the overall group. **Pre-registered decision threshold:** H1 is **SUPPORTED** if Xi stands in the central front-row position or one position to either side of it, **OR** his normalised horizontal position within the image's occupied group width is **0.40–0.60 inclusive** (leftmost participant centre = 0.00, rightmost participant centre = 1.00; report the measured value, frame, and denominator). Otherwise H1 is **DISCONFIRMED**, provided the full front row and participant positions are observable. **NOT ASSESSABLE:** no authenticated usable full-group frame, ambiguous identity or undeterminable front row/group bounds. Independently record Trump and Putin's positions without assuming adjacency. This concerns visible positioning, not geopolitical power or intent.

**H2 - Pre-positioned visual choreography hypothesis (comparative link to PN-010; DESCRIPTIVE / LOW-RISK, not a strong causal test).** Where authentic, sufficiently detailed pre-event or in-event photographs show floor-position indicators (tape, marks, plaques or guides), the indicators are predicted to correspond to at least two occupied standing places in the same scene. **Test:** match identifiable visible markers and subsequent foot positions using temporally attributable frames; report both count and alignment. **Disconfirmation:** visible markers systematically do not align with occupied positions in comparable frames. **Not visible** is NOT evidence that markers were absent. PN-010 is a distinct Manila case used only as a methodological comparator; its outcome may not be imported into WP-016.

**H3 - Triadic spatial parity hypothesis (bilateral-to-trilateral comparison).** If comparable authenticated imagery from both meetings is available, the trilateral staging is predicted to show balanced formal representation of the two visiting principals. **Only four fixed indicators count:** (a) **seat type**, (b) **flag presence**, (c) **nameplate presence/type**, and (d) **position relative to the head of the table**. **Visitor privilege / asymmetry** is operationally present if an indicator is absent for one visitor but present for the other, or is visibly of a different type for one visitor. Code each indicator separately as SAME / DIFFERENT / NOT VISIBLE / UNAVAILABLE. For relative position, DIFFERENT means one visitor visibly occupies a privileged head-of-table position while the other does not; a purely different left/right side does not establish privilege by itself. **SUPPORTED:** at least two independently visible comparable indicators and no visitor asymmetry among available indicators. **DISCONFIRMED:** at least one clear asymmetry in an available indicator, accompanied by a documented frame/location and independently reviewable comparison. **NOT ASSESSABLE:** fewer than two comparable indicators or insufficient authenticated imagery. Do not infer substantive equality or bargaining authority.

**H4 - Non-tautological format-boundary signalling hypothesis.** If both bilateral and trilateral events proceed and each yields authenticated, comparable public visual material, the transition will be identifiable through **at least TWO changes in the following fixed non-automatic variables only:** (a) **backdrop**, (b) **room**, (c) **table geometry**, (d) **host position**. **EXCLUDED from the H4 change count:** the number of principals and any identifiers automatically required by the presence of a third principal, including a third seat, third flag and third nameplate. Those facts may be documented elsewhere, but cannot confirm H4. **SUPPORTED:** two or more clearly observed changes from the fixed four-variable set. **DISCONFIRMED:** sufficiently comparable material shows fewer than two changes when all four variables can be reliably coded. **NOT ASSESSABLE:** one event cancelled or lacking imagery, or material cannot show enough of the four-variable set to judge the threshold. Never redefine variables after seeing the events. The test concerns public ceremonial staging, not political agreement.

## Frozen coding safeguards

1. **Evidence ladder:** Official dated schedule > verified first-party imagery/video > attributed press-pool imagery > reliable secondary reporting. Mark FACT, VISUAL OBSERVATION, WPA ANALYTICAL NOTE and LIMITATION separately. Cross-check altered/edited frames; photographs alone cannot prove intent, private agreements, or legal mandate.
2. **Missingness:** Distinguish `NOT_VISIBLE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNVERIFIED`, `CONFIRMED_ABSENT` and `CANCELLED`; do not convert any unknown into a zero or a disconfirmation. No computer-generated reconstruction or image enhancement may be presented as primary evidence.
3. **Schedule deviation:** If a meeting shifts, preserve the original event identifier and add actual time. If shortened, code SHORTENED and only observed elements. If cancelled, code CANCELLED and mark meeting-dependent hypotheses NOT ASSESSABLE; cancellation is an important recorded outcome, not an invitation to change hypotheses. Report any added unplanned event separately as EXPLORATORY Phase B.
4. **Temporal non-contamination:** Arrivals, welcome encounters and any events on 18 November are EXCLUDED from the confirmatory H1-H4 dataset. They may be discussed as context in Phase B only, clearly labelled as known after A0 lock. APEC family photograph on 19 November IS INCLUDED as a comparative observation. No interpretation or predictions may be revised after seeing footage.
5. **Version integrity and public lock:** Independent QA may propose corrections; every revision receives a new SHA-256, with the old hash kept in the version history. Final Human Gate must explicitly approve the exact bytes. The approved file is to be committed to a **public GitHub repository** and its SHA-256 posted publicly by the author on LinkedIn (or an equally independently time-stamped public channel) **before the declared evidence cut-off**. Save the public post URL, GitHub commit SHA, UTC timestamp and local timestamp. A GitHub draft commit alone is not proof of a final human-approved lock. Do **not** create a separate Zenodo record for A0; append to the later WP-016 publication record to preserve corpus counts.
6. **Readout and imagery temporal cut-off:** Any APEC/arrival material already visible before the actual lock must be disclosed in a contamination register with URL and first-known time. Do not use it to edit predictions. Any Phase A1 after this file may specify coding implementation without modifying H1-H4/O01-O12; violations must be disclosed as exploratory changes, not silently rewritten.
