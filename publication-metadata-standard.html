WPA Publication Metadata Standard

This standard governs future and corrected metadata for WPA Working Papers, Protocol Notes and comparable public research records. It does not retroactively rewrite historically accurate statements inside published works.

FieldCanonical rule
Series identifierUse a stable form such as WPA-WP-016 or WPA-PN-010 and keep the series identity explicit even where a repository's generic resource type differs.
TitleOne canonical title per version; identifier placement should be consistent within the series.
AuthorUse one canonical author display form and link ORCID where available and authorised.
VersionUse one version convention for future deposits; avoid mixing v2, 1.9.2, v14 and “FINAL DOI-LOCKED” as equivalent version semantics.
DateDistinguish publication date, upload/deposit date and revision date where they differ.
LanguageDeclare the record language and link parallel-language editions without implying identity where content differs.
LicenceState the applicable licence explicitly.
Review statusState whether the item is author-reviewed, editorially reviewed, peer-reviewed or not formally peer-reviewed. DOI does not prove review status.
Community / publisherUse a consistent WPA community/publisher naming convention.
ProvenanceRecord source/version history and material correction or replacement notes.

Historical-state rule

A dated statement that accurately described the corpus at its publication date remains historical evidence and is not changed merely because later items were added. Metadata corrections may be made when the metadata itself is inaccurate, incomplete or inconsistent, with a visible revision trail where the repository supports it.
