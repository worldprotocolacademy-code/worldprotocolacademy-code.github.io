Protocolometry
Scientific Standard

Протоколометриски научен стандард — работна методологија со јасни правила за доказ, оценување, ограничувања и човечка ревизија.

Candidate, authorial methodology for evidence-bounded protocol decision education. Not independently validated; no institution ranking or credential follows from this document.

1. Scope · Опфат

Unit of analysis: a learner's selection in a fictional, pre-scripted decision step. The unit is not the competence, integrity, legal compliance or international standing of a person, state, institution or diplomatic mission.

Scope: Three bounded teaching scenarios (state visit, international conference and a protocol-related incident), each with three multiple-choice steps. The source-anchored Atlas informs themes; it does not imply that the imagined choices happened during any documented event.

Проверлива научна граница: „Протоколот е апсолутен“ останува авторска максима во Смиљанова синтеза. Не следува автоматски дека апсолутното е мерливо. Мерливи се само однапред дефинирани, контекстуални и проверливи индикатори со јавно наведени ограничувања.

2. Evidence taxonomy · Доказни состојби

FACT / Documented

Official-source statements describing an event or institutional practice. Checked against a cited public page. They are not independently peer-reviewed by WPA in this pilot.

WPA ANALYTICAL QUESTION

WPA-authored teaching prompt derived from a documented situation or rule. It is not a historical claim.

SYNTHETIC SCENARIO

Entirely fictional sequence used for structured learning. No real-world event, confidential information or professional evaluation is implied.

Further evidence statuses: VERIFIED requires attributable source, date and record-level review; CORROBORATED requires independent confirmation; NOT EVIDENCED means insufficient support, which is distinct from disproved or zero. All 12 Atlas entries currently carry SOURCE_ANCHORED_EDITORIAL_REVIEW_PENDING. Source provenance is traceable but independent review remains a Human Gate.

3. Four-dimensional pilot rubric · 0–4

Dimension / ДимензијаWhat is observed
NORM · Applicable normsRecognises the applicable institutional context, authorities and protocol requirements; avoids cross-context rule transfer.
EVID · EvidenceChecks a primary source, dates, titles, evidence limitations and version before making a claim.
COORD · CoordinationConsults authorised parties, avoids unilateral changes, minimises unnecessary disclosure and documents communication.
H-GATE · Human responsibilityRecognises who must approve, correct, escalate or authorise consequential institutional action.

Per-choice rubric

0 — contradicts the dimension or bypasses a required authority; 1 — substantial gap; 2 — partial action without adequate evidence or coordination; 3 — largely aligned but with a specific omission; 4 — best-supported pedagogical response within the fictional options.

Calculation (pilot only): three questions × four dimensions × four maximum points = 48. Equal weights (25% per dimension) are applied. Overall displayed value: round(100 × sum(points) / 48). Each dimension: round(100 × dimension points / 12). The scores are fixed editorial choices, not learned or independently psychometrically calibrated weights.

Missing data: An unanswered question is NOT EVIDENCED, not automatically 0. The interface cannot complete/score until all three questions are answered. A selected low-value response may receive zero under the rubric; that is distinct from missingness.

Interpretation: A score is a teaching feedback index about choices in one bounded fictional task. It cannot predict actual professional performance or confer competence, a qualification, legal compliance, employment eligibility, accreditation or any form of institutional ranking.

4. Inclusion, provenance and sampling

The initial Atlas contains six dated state visits recorded in an official Royal Household list and six institutional/treaty/IOC practice references. Inclusion requires an identifiable public primary source, bounded factual statement and separated educational question. Exclusion applies to unattributed anecdotes, rumours, inferred private motives, unsupported institutional quality judgements and content requiring nonpublic personal data.

Sampling bias: This pilot deliberately over-represents the UK state-visit chronology and includes only four official source families. It is not geographically or institutionally representative of world protocol practice. Future case expansion must report geographic/language coverage and alternative selection strategies before any comparative claim.

5. Review, bias and reliability gates

Evidence audit

Record source URL, issuing body, access date, exact claim, scope and correction/version history. Verify citation links and the absence of invented incidents or quotes.

Editorial and COI

Separate authorial design from external review. A reviewer with material conflicts must recuse; the same person must not both author and independently approve a contested scoring rule.

Inter-rater reliability

Future work: independent blind scoring of the same hypothetical answers; predefine agreement statistic, sample size and acceptable uncertainty before declaring reliability. Not completed in v0.1.

Sensitivity

Future work: recompute with alternative defensible dimension weights; publish whether teaching conclusions change and identify missing/inconsistent evidence. Not completed in v0.1.

Fairness & accessibility

Test language parity, disability access, cultural-context misapplication, scenario assumptions, keyboard operation and whether the same response receives consistent feedback.

External review

Independent subject experts should review the rubric and its limitations. Until an actual review occurs, keep the status candidate/pilot.

6. Human Gate and corrections

The simulator does not make binding decisions, collect user identities or send responses to a server. Results can be downloaded locally. A source revision, disputed interpretation or significant error requires editorial correction with version and provenance, followed by Human Gate before public release.

Official WPA credentials, badges and registry functions remain FROZEN / NOT ISSUING. Open Badges 3.0 compatibility would require separate issuer identity, learner consent, verification, legal basis, revocation, cryptographic proof, privacy assessment and formal release approval. See credential readiness dossier.
