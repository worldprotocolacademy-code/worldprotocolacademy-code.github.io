WPA bibliographic summary

This paper compares the institutional and symbolic continuity of Macedonian diplomatic protocol across three historical periods. It uses a nine-parameter comparative matrix and introduces the “Protocol Thread” as an analytical method for tracing four protocol symbols—credentials, the flag, the formula of representation and the reception ceremony—across political transformation. The paper argues that protocol functions as a durable institutional framework connecting diplomatic identity, sovereignty, integration and continuity.
