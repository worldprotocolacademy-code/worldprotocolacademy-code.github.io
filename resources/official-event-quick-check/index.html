WPA Official Event Quick Check

Use these 12 checks before an official visit, ceremony, conference or institutional event is locked. The goal is simple: catch avoidable errors before they become visible.

1

Mandate & host role

Confirm who is formally hosting, who has decision authority and which institutional protocol applies.

2

Names, titles & forms of address

Verify official names, ranks, titles, honorifics and pronunciation from competent sources.

3

Order of precedence

Check the applicable precedence framework before seating, speaking order, arrivals and photo lines are finalised.

4

Flags & state symbols

Verify the correct flags, proportions, order, placement and any host-specific display rules.

5

Anthem version & sequence

Confirm the authorised recording or performance version, duration, language where relevant and ceremonial sequence.

6

Arrival & departure sequence

Map principals, greeters, vehicles, holding points, escorts and fallback timing.

7

Seating & positioning

Validate table plans, stage positions, bilateral arrangements, signing positions and accessibility.

8

Nameplates, signage & languages

Proofread spelling, transliteration, titles, flags, directional signage and language order.

9

Speaking order & scripts

Confirm speakers, introductions, acknowledgements, timing and the final approved running order.

10

Media & visual statecraft

Check camera lines, backgrounds, symbols, lecterns, photo opportunities and what the official image will communicate.

11

Security, accessibility & contingency

Coordinate public-facing protocol with authorised security, medical, accessibility and technical contingency teams.

12

Final human verification

Before execution, re-check time-sensitive facts and institutional decisions against the competent official authority. Content is not command; access is not mandate.
