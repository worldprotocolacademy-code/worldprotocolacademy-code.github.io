Evidence rules

Tier A · Primary / official

Official institutional pages/documents, government or legal registries, official repositories, DOI/ISBN/ISSN or equivalent primary metadata.

Tier B · Authoritative external

Recognised scholarly/bibliographic indexes, international organisations, established academic databases or independent registries.

Tier C · Reputable secondary

Reputable universities, research organisations, established professional publications and credible secondary documentation.

Tier D · Uncorroborated / promotional

Marketing copy, social posts, unverified directories or unsupported partnership claims. Tier D alone cannot support a score above 2.

Core rule: follower counts, likes and general popularity are not academic or professional quality indicators. URL presence alone does not equal verification.
