Global Institutional Evidence Programme

A human-governed, public-evidence programme for discovering the global field, extracting institutional practice, routing approved institutional DNA across WPA, validating the WPA 20-indicator methodology and controlling consequential publication.

Public evidence only. AI-supported agents may discover, extract, map and propose. They may not bypass access controls, invent missing data or create authoritative institutional scores, accreditation, recognition or final rankings. Consequential decisions remain human-governed.

Four operating pillars

P1

Global Coverage Expansion

REV3 remains the frozen baseline. New diplomatic academies, foreign-service institutes and protocol schools enter a REV4 candidate queue and pass entity resolution, primary-source verification and human review before canonical inclusion.

P2

Institutional Core Extraction Engine

For every resolved institution, WPA builds a structured Evidence Dossier from lawful public sources and converts approved findings into source-traceable Institutional Practice Atoms.

P3

Methodology Validation

The global corpus tests I01–I20 through practice coverage, independent double scoring, weight sensitivity, correlation/redundancy, peer-type fairness, evidence coverage and correction testing.

P4

Human Governance & Publication Gate

Human review controls canonical inclusion, Practice Atom reuse, final scoring, corrections, COI decisions, methodology freeze and any future public comparative or ordinal output.

Current implementation

Baseline: Master List REV3 remains frozen and reproducible.

Coverage: REV4 Global Coverage Pass v1 now spans Europe/Balkans, MENA, Africa, Asia-Pacific and Latin America/Caribbean. Wave 1 and the global pass together contain 51 candidate or entity-review cases. These do not alter canonical REV3 headline counts.

Evidence: Six source-bounded first-pass dossiers are active: Singapore, Poland, Qatar, Kenya, Thailand and Peru. Every dossier maps public evidence to I01–I20 and uses NE when support is insufficient. All remain human-review pending and carry no final score.

Reuse: Approved evidence can be atomised into Institutional Practice Atoms and routed across eligible WPA domains without losing provenance, uncertainty or source context.

Queue: 38 cases are in the next-priority dossier queue; 7 require additional entity/currentness/source resolution before dossiering.

Validation: A ten-stage methodology validation protocol remains mandatory before v1.0.

Agent workflow

Discovery Agent — finds candidates and probable official sources.
Entity-Resolution Agent — checks aliases, branches, successors, mergers and duplicate contexts.
Evidence Agent — extracts only source-supported facts into the dossier.
Indicator-Mapping Agent — maps evidence to I01–I20 and records NE where evidence is insufficient.
Methodology-Learning Agent — identifies distinctive practices and methodology gaps without forcing them into existing indicators.
Practice-Atom Routing Agent — proposes bounded reuse routes across WPA while preserving attribution, context and Human Gate requirements.
QA / Contradiction Agent — searches for stale, conflicting, duplicated or unsupported claims before human review.

Institutional DNA → the whole WPA Institute

Reuse boundary: WPA learns from verified practices; it does not copy proprietary curricula, reproduce substantial protected material, claim external practice as WPA authorship, or imply partnership/endorsement. Corrections to source evidence must propagate to dependent WPA outputs where materially relevant.

Access and ethics boundary

Allowed: public institutional websites, ministries, public PDFs, curricula, annual reports, public repositories, DOI/ISBN/ISSN metadata, public faculty/governance pages, public partnership notices and authoritative external sources.

Not allowed: login bypass, paywall/CAPTCHA/access-control circumvention, leaked or guessed credentials, closed student/staff portals, autonomous impersonation/contact, private-data collection, hidden-data inference or invented missing values.

Missing evidence rule: NE = Not Evidenced. NE is not zero, not a penalty and not an estimate.

Methodology learning loop

The first extraction pass asks what each institution actually does, not where it ranks. Only after the institutional core is documented does WPA map practices to I01–I20. Repeated high-value practices that are not covered become methodology-review triggers. Repeated overlap becomes a redundancy-review trigger.

The target is not a predetermined number of institutions. The target is a defensible global corpus with transparent evidence, entity resolution, governed reuse and correction rights.
